## 4. FAQ Block (restructured from the 5 "Objection Buster" cards → true FAQPage Q&A)

> Executed §3 **FAQ Schema Content Creator** + **Featured Snippet Optimizer**. KEEP the 5 existing objection
> topics, rewrite as clean Q&A (no curly-quote phrasing — §4 NEVER), ≤2–3 sentences each, FAQPage-schema-ready.
> Added one banking Q (Q6) to seat the sub-narrative + the internal link. Brand voice: consultative, specific, no hype.

**Q1. How does a real estate agent earn $3,500+ per client?**
You refer the buyer's after-sale needs — UAE banking, visa, company setup — to WTP. We handle delivery and pay you commission on each service the client takes, which averages $3,500+ per client across the bundle.

**Q2. What does it cost me or my client?**
Nothing changes for you: no fee, no upfront cost, no extra workload. The client pays standard service rates they would pay any provider; your referral simply routes that spend through WTP so you earn on it.

**Q3. How much of my time does this take?**
About three WhatsApp messages. You introduce the client, we take over the banking, visa and setup work, and you stay informed without managing the process.

**Q4. Will WTP take my client or sell them property?**
No. WTP does not broker property and does not compete for your listings or future deals. We deliver the post-deal banking and relocation services only; the client relationship stays yours.

**Q5. What if the client's bank account gets rejected?**
We run a Pre-Screen Go/No-Go before any application, so the client knows the odds before paying. UAE banks reject 50–65% of non-resident and SME accounts without the right structure — screening first is how we avoid that.

**Q6. Do my buyers actually need a bank account after buying property?** *(banking sub-narrative)*
Yes. A Dubai property purchase doesn't open a UAE bank account, secure a visa, or set up a company — buyers need those next, and banking is the step that fails most often. That is exactly the after-sale work you refer and earn on. See [how WTP opens UAE bank accounts](https://banking.wtp.ae/).

**Featured-snippet target (list format) for "what services do dubai property buyers need after the deal":**
seed a 3-item ordered answer block directly under that H2 — `1. UAE bank account (the gating step) · 2. Residency / Golden Visa · 3. Company setup & asset structuring` — short, list-shaped, snippet-eligible.
