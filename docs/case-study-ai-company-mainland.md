# Case Study: Launching an AI Technology Company on Dubai Mainland

---

## Client Background

The client came to WTP through a referral from a long-standing partner — the owner of one of the largest multi-family offices in Spain.

A year prior, the client had invested in two talented developers working in artificial intelligence and wanted to create a full corporate structure in the UAE for the project.

**Primary objectives:**
- Register a company that could operate on the international market
- Simultaneously serve clients directly in Dubai

The decision was made to register on **Dubai Mainland**, which provides maximum flexibility for working with:
- Local companies
- Government projects
- International clients

At first glance, the case appeared straightforward. WTP registers similar companies regularly, and the process typically goes quickly without complications.

However, during the preliminary document check, it became clear that the project would be significantly more complex than it appeared.

---

## Initial Challenge: Visa Restrictions

One of the project participants held a Tajikistan passport that had not previously been used to enter the UAE.

WTP initiated the standard tourist visa procedure so the owner could fly in and personally participate in the registration process. At this point, an unexpected external restriction emerged.

Shortly before this, UAE immigration authorities had temporarily tightened visa policy for citizens of this country, resulting in mass rejections of tourist visa applications — including the client's application.

**This created a risk of completely halting the project launch.**

---

## Strategic Solution

To avoid delaying the business launch, WTP proposed an alternative legal model:

- Arranged a Power of Attorney
- Conducted **full company registration remotely**
- Used documents of the second owner with citizenship of another country

After registration was completed, the owner was able to enter the UAE on an **Entry Permit** through the company partner, bypassing the tourist visa route entirely.

**This preserved the project launch timeline and avoided additional visa risks.**

---

## Immigration Process

After the client arrived in the UAE, WTP immediately began the residency visa procedure.

### Biometric System Failure
During the procedure, a rare technical failure occurred: the client's biometric data (fingerprints) were not correctly uploaded to the immigration system.

WTP response:
- Organized a repeat biometric procedure at a different center
- Ensured correct data entry into the system

### Emirates ID Delay
Later, another issue arose — the Emirates ID was delayed in the system due to a technical glitch.

WTP contacted the main immigration center directly and obtained the card **the same day**, so the client could depart according to his schedule.

---

## Banking Setup

After completing immigration procedures, WTP organized:
- **Corporate bank account** opening
- **Personal bank account** opening for the owner
- Online banking setup

Banking cards were sent to the company owner via DHL to his permanent residence.

---

## Operational Support (Ongoing)

WTP's work did not end with company registration. **For over a year**, the team continued supporting the project with operational business tasks:

- Communication with banks
- Card unblocking assistance
- International service payments
- Employee onboarding (visa, contracts)
- Accounting organization
- Various administrative support

The development team had strong technological expertise but no experience with the UAE banking system.

> In essence, WTP became the project's **operational business infrastructure.**

---

## Long-Term Relationship

Over time, the client entrusted WTP with additional tasks:
- Real estate investments in Dubai
- Various administrative and operational matters

Many of these tasks were handled not only within the formal contract but through the long-term partnership relationship.

---

## Result

**The client received:**
- Fully functioning AI company on Dubai Mainland
- Corporate and personal banking infrastructure
- Residency visa
- Stable operational support

The company has been successfully operating for almost a year, and WTP continues to support the project, including preparation for license renewal.

---

## Key Takeaway

> Launching a business in the UAE is not just company registration — it's the ability to find solutions in complex situations and accompany the client at every stage of business development.

What started as a "simple registration" turned into a year-long partnership: visa workarounds, biometric fixes, banking setup, employee onboarding, and real estate investments — all handled by one team.
