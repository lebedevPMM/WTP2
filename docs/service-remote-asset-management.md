# WTP Service: Remote Asset Management & Operations Outside UAE

*Supporting clients during geopolitical uncertainty*

---

## Context

Recent Middle East events and rising tensions around the Iran–Israel–US conflict are causing natural concern among investors in the UAE.

Some clients consider returning to Russia or Europe. Others want to lock in profits on real estate deals. But all of them share one thing: **the desire to shift asset management to a remote mode.**

**Key message:** In most cases, there is no need to urgently sell assets or close businesses. Many processes can be safely and legally organized remotely. This is exactly the situation WTP launched a dedicated support format for.

---

## What WTP Can Do Remotely

### 1. Real Estate Purchase & Sale Without Owner Present

Full transaction management through POA (Power of Attorney):
- Remote POA arrangement
- Representation at Dubai Land Department
- All document signing
- Payment receipt and transfer

WTP can officially receive sale proceeds to its UAE accounts and transfer them to the client in a convenient jurisdiction.

### 2. Property Management

If the client doesn't want to sell:
- Tenant management and rental
- Rent collection
- Service charge payments
- Property management
- Property insurance

WTP acts as the local representative of the owner.

### 3. Banking Infrastructure Management

- Resolving issues with banks
- Card renewals
- Account unblocking
- International payments
- Service and invoice payments

No physical presence required in the UAE.

### 4. Company Management

For clients with UAE businesses:
- License renewal
- Accounting maintenance
- Bank communication
- Administrative issue management
- Operational support

### 5. Preparing for Possible Return

For clients who temporarily leave, WTP helps preserve infrastructure:
- Residency visas
- Bank accounts
- Companies
- Assets

So the client can return at any moment and continue without losing the structure.

## WTP's Approach

> We do not recommend making emotional decisions.

The geopolitical situation can change very quickly, and often the wiser path is to:
- Preserve assets
- Organize remote management
- Calmly evaluate next steps

**WTP's mission:** Ensure clients maintain control over their assets and peace of mind, regardless of where they are physically located. And if necessary — diversify jurisdictions.
