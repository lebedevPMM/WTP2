## Block 1: UK Client Compliance Filters

### Why banks tightened filters for UK clients

- Sanctions perimeter
- Tax transparency (CRS / HMRC)
- Crypto AML
- "Paper residency" structures

### 1. Key Bank Compliance Questions for UK Clients

#### a) Tax Residency & Real Center of Life

Banks ask:
- Where is the client tax resident?
- Where is the center of economic interests?
- UK Self-Assessment filings status
- Business ties to UK (clients, office, team)

**Red flag:** Client says "moved to UAE" but business remains in UK.

UK actively checks economic substance. UAE banks don't want to participate in structures that look like tax avoidance relocation schemes.

#### b) Source of Wealth vs Source of Funds

Banks now strictly separate:
- **Source of Wealth (SoW):** Where accumulated assets come from (business sale, property, inheritance)
- **Source of Funds (SoF):** Where current money comes from (salary, dividends, rental income)

Typical document requests:
- Sales contracts
- Dividend history
- Tax payments
- Audited financial statements

#### c) Crypto Income / Crypto Activity

With UK clients, banks almost always ask:
> "Did any part of the wealth originate from cryptocurrency?"

They check:
- Which exchanges
- AML trace
- Conversion history
- Wallet ownership

**Red flag:** Crypto > 20-30% of total wealth → additional scrutiny.

### 2. UK Documents That Trigger Questions

#### a) Business sale (5-10 years ago)

If client sold a company (e.g., in 2014), bank requests:
- Sale & Purchase Agreement
- Payment confirmation
- Bank statement from that period
- Tax return confirming tax was paid

**No documents = red flag.**

#### b) Dividends from UK companies

Compliance asks:
- Companies House filings
- Dividend vouchers
- Shareholder register

**Problem:** Dividends often not reflected in tax returns.

#### c) Crypto-to-fiat conversions

Required:
- Exchange confirmation / account statement
- Wallet history
- Transaction proof

**Red flag:** DeFi / mixers / unidentifiable wallets.

### 3. Newest Bank Filter (2025-2026)

Compliance increasingly asks:
> "Why do you need an account if activity remains in UK?"

If client can't demonstrate UAE-based clients, suppliers, or planned investments → account may be denied.

### Top 5 Red Flags for UK Clients

1. **Paper relocation** from UK without real substance
2. **Business sale** without documented money trail
3. **Crypto assets** without AML documentation
4. **Complex offshore structures** without economic logic
5. **No UAE-based business activity** planned

## Block 2: Case Study — "Company Exists, No Bank Account"

### Background

Client opened a Free Zone company 4 months ago through another agency. License: General Trading. After 3 bank rejections and 2 non-responses, client came to WTP.

### What Compliance Saw

**Mistake #1: Wrong license logic**

License said: General Trading
Client actually planned: electronics trading, equipment trading, investments, asset management

When answering bank questions about business activities, client described plans without a structured business model. For the bank → "payment vehicle company" = **red flag**.

**Mistake #2: No operational business logic**

Client couldn't provide:
- List of clients and suppliers
- Production and goods movement chain
- Planned contracts

For the bank → non-viable business model.

**Mistake #3: Company opened BEFORE banking pre-check**

The biggest mistake of inexperienced/unscrupulous agencies:
1. Register company
2. Hand over documents to client
3. Client is stuck trying to open an account with a predictably negative outcome

The company was **non-bankable from day one**.

### What WTP Had to Fix

1. Coordinate planned changes with banking structures per all norms
2. Change business activities on license
3. Prepare business model memo for the bank
4. Confirm source of wealth
5. Show potential first counterparties for contracts

**Result:** Account opened at a UAE bank.

### Key Takeaway

> The mistake wasn't the banks. The mistake was that the client was sold a **company setup** instead of a **bankable structure**.

## Block 3: Economic Substance — What Banks Really Check

Banks verify **economic substance** of the company, not just the license. Today you sell not "company setup" but a structure that actually passes banking compliance.

### Legally sufficient (but not for banks):
- License
- Flexi desk
- Emirates ID

### What top UAE banks actually check (Emirates NBD, FAB, ADCB):

**Main compliance question:**
> Where does the business actually operate?
> What exactly does the business operate?

### Minimum Requirements for UK Holding / Trading Company

#### 1. Real Office (not flexi desk)
Bank requests:
- Lease agreement / Ejari
- Address for bank visit
- Office photos and area
- Utility bills: DEWA, telecom, service charges

#### 2. Staff
- Employee or active resident director
- Labour contract
- Visa
- Potential hiring plan

#### 3. Operational Activity
Banks want to see:
- Contracts, invoices, supply agreements, sales contracts
- Full chain: procurement → production → logistics → sales

#### 4. Transaction Logic
Bank analyzes:
- Payment geography
- Currencies
- Type of operations

If UK ↔ EU transactions planned, compliance asks:
> "Why is this company located in the UAE?"

### Critical 2026 Nuance

Banks increasingly check alignment of three elements:
1. **License** activities
2. **Real operations**
3. **Account transactions**

If mismatched (e.g., license says "electronics trading" but payments come for sports goods / furniture / metals / real estate / cars) → compliance questions → account likely blocked.

## Block 4: Bank-Fit Map UAE

### Segment: UK Non-Dom / HNW Individuals
### Purpose: Personal capital transfer for investments (real estate / portfolio / family office)

### Friendly Banks

#### 1. Emirates NBD

Tiers (by practice, not official):
| Tier | Min AED | Min USD |
|------|---------|---------|
| Priority | 500K AED | ~$135K |
| Lower Private | ~5M AED | ~$1.35M |
| Core Private | ~10M AED | ~$2.7M |
| Ultra / Family Office | 30-50M AED+ | ~$8-14M+ |

**Why good:**
- Likes transparent Non-Dom / UK tax resident structure
- Clear Source of Wealth
- Clear investment goal
- Strong experience with investment accounts

**Timeline:** 2-3 weeks, faster through Private banker

**Typical route:** personal account → investment account → international transfers

#### 2. Mashreq Bank

Tiers:
| Tier | Min USD |
|------|---------|
| Mashreq Gold | ~$250K |
| Mashreq Private Banking | ~$1M |

**Why often used:**
- More flexible onboarding
- Good experience with UK clients
- Fast KYC

**Timeline:** 2-3 weeks

**Advantage:** Often faster than other banks at start

#### 3. First Abu Dhabi Bank (FAB)

Tiers:
| Tier | Min USD |
|------|---------|
| FAB Elite | ~$135K |
| FAB Private Banking | ~$2M |

**Why good:**
- Major regional bank
- Strong HNW track record
- Best for family office / wealth relocation

**Timeline:** 2-3 weeks

**Likes:** Investment profile, long-term capital relocation

### Banks That Often Reject

#### Standard Chartered UAE
- **Problem:** Very strict UK-risk policy
- **Typical rejection reasons:** Unclear relocation, UK tax exposure
- **Result:** Prolonged KYC → rejection

#### Citibank UAE
- **Problem:** Works almost exclusively with existing global clients
- **New client onboarding:** Effectively closed

### What Banks Ask UK Non-Dom Clients

**Source of Wealth:**
- Sale of business
- Dividend history
- Property sales

**Documents:**
- UK tax return
- Companies House records
- SPA (Sale & Purchase Agreement)

**Account purpose:**
- Investment
- Asset diversification
- UAE residence

### Quick Bank-Fit Summary

| Category | Banks |
|----------|-------|
| **Best options** | Emirates NBD, Mashreq, FAB |
| **Case-by-case** | ADCB, HSBC, ADIB |
| **Difficult** | Standard Chartered, Citibank |
