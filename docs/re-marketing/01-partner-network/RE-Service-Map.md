# WTP Real Estate Service Map

**End-to-end client journey from discovery to post-purchase management.**

This map outlines what WTP does, what the partner does, timeline expectations, and deliverables at each phase.

---

## Phase 1: Discovery

**Objective:** Understand client profile, investment goals, budget, and timeline.

**What WTP does:**
- Initial questionnaire: investment purpose (residence/yield/Golden Visa), budget range, risk tolerance, preferred areas
- Client profile documentation: nationality, current residency, Source of Funds overview (high-level)
- Banking scenario discussion: types of banks client has worked with, any previous UAE banking attempts

**What partner does:**
- Client referral with context: "UK tax advisor referring client relocating due to non-dom changes, budget $2-3M, needs Golden Visa + substance"
- Provide background on client's tax situation, home jurisdiction exit planning status
- Clarify if client is ready to proceed or in research phase

**Timeline:** 1-2 days (questionnaire completion, initial call)

**Deliverables:**
- WTP: Case acknowledgment (4 hours), discovery call scheduled
- Partner: Client profile summary sent to WTP

---

## Phase 2: Pre-Screen (Banking Eligibility & Risk Assessment)

**Objective:** Determine if client can open UAE bank account BEFORE property commitment.

**What WTP does:**
- Request detailed SOF documentation: bank statements (6 months), source of wealth narrative, corporate ownership structures if applicable
- KYC/AML review: sanctions screening, PEP check, jurisdiction risk assessment
- Banking scenario modeling: which UAE banks match client profile (tier-1 vs. tier-2, international vs. local)
- Risk classification: Green (proceed), Yellow (conditional — requires enhanced due diligence), Red (decline)
- Pre-screen report: banking options, estimated account opening timeline, document requirements, structure recommendations

**What partner does:**
- Assist client in gathering SOF documents
- Provide context on any complex SOF elements (e.g., corporate sale proceeds, inheritance, crypto conversion)
- Review pre-screen report with client, address questions on banking scenario

**Timeline:** Initial verdict (Green/Yellow/Red): 48 hours. Full pre-screen report (PDF, 5-8 pages): 5-7 business days from complete document submission.

**Deliverables:**
- WTP: Pre-screen report (PDF, 5-8 pages) — banking verdict, risk classification, recommended next steps
- Partner: Client sign-off to proceed (if Green/Yellow) or exit conversation (if Red)

**Key decision point:** If banking scenario is Red or client rejects Yellow conditions, project ends here. No property search initiated. This protects client from purchasing property they cannot bank around.

---

## Phase 3: Property Selection

**Objective:** Identify property aligned to client goals, budget, and banking/visa requirements.

**What WTP does:**
- Area analysis: present 3-5 shortlisted areas based on criteria (ROI, Golden Visa eligibility, lifestyle, banking signal to banks)
- Developer due diligence: financial stability check for off-plan developers, delivery track record, Golden Visa approval status
- Property shortlist: 5-10 options with analysis (price per sqft, rental yield estimate, proximity to amenities, title status)
- Viewing coordination: schedule viewings, accompany client, provide on-site analysis
- Market data provision: comparable sales, price trends, area development plans

**What partner does:**
- Advise client on structure implications: "If you buy in personal name, UK tax reporting applies. If via UAE company, different treatment. Discuss with me before making offer."
- Review property shortlist with client if client requests partner input
- Confirm client's decision to proceed with specific property

**Timeline:** 1-3 weeks (depends on client availability for viewings, decision speed)

**Deliverables:**
- WTP: Area analysis report, property shortlist (Excel/PDF), viewing summaries, market data pack
- Partner: Structure advisory note to client (if applicable)

---

## Phase 4: Transaction (Due Diligence → SPA → Close)

**Objective:** Execute property purchase with full legal and financial diligence.

**What WTP does:**
- **Due diligence (3-5 days):**
  - Title verification: confirm seller ownership, check encumbrances, verify DLD records
  - Developer financials: for off-plan, verify escrow account, construction progress, Oqood eligibility
  - SPA review: contract terms, payment schedule, handover conditions, penalties
  - Mortgage coordination if applicable: pre-approval, bank selection, documentation
- **Offer and negotiation (1-3 days):**
  - Submit offer on behalf of client, negotiate price/terms
  - Draft MOU (Memorandum of Understanding) if seller requires pre-SPA commitment
- **SPA signing and deposit (1-2 days):**
  - Coordinate SPA signing (in-person or POA if client remote)
  - Transfer deposit to escrow (typically 10% of purchase price)
  - Receive signed SPA and deposit receipt
- **Transaction close (1-2 weeks):**
  - Coordinate full payment transfer (from client account → seller/developer escrow)
  - DLD registration: Trustee Office appointment, submit forms, pay DLD fees (4% + admin)
  - Title deed issuance (for ready property) or Oqood certificate (for off-plan)
  - Handover coordination: key collection, snagging inspection (defect checklist)

**What partner does:**
- Review SPA on client's behalf if client requests (or recommend independent legal review)
- Confirm client authorization for deposit and full payment transfers
- Advise on any tax reporting requirements in home jurisdiction (e.g., UK clients must report foreign property on tax return)

**Timeline:** 2-4 weeks from offer to title deed in hand

**Deliverables:**
- WTP: Due diligence report, negotiated SPA, title deed or Oqood certificate, transaction summary with all receipts
- Partner: Tax reporting advisory (if applicable in client's jurisdiction)

## Phase 5: Infrastructure (Company, Bank Account, Visa)

**Objective:** Build operational infrastructure around property ownership.

**What WTP does:**
- **Ownership structure setup (if needed, 1-2 weeks):**
  - If client chose company ownership route: register UAE LLC or Free Zone company
  - Corporate bank account opening for company (if property held in company name)
  - Director appointment, MOA/AOA drafting, initial share allocation
- **Personal bank account opening (2-4 weeks):**
  - Submit application to pre-selected bank (from Phase 2 banking scenario)
  - Coordinate client meeting with bank (in-person or via POA if remote onboarding available)
  - Follow up on account approval, card issuance, online banking activation
- **Golden Visa processing (6-8 weeks, if property is 2M+ AED):**
  - Application submission: title deed, passport copies, Emirates ID application, proof of relationship for dependents
  - Medical fitness test coordination: approved clinics, results submission
  - Biometrics appointment: Emirates ID photo and fingerprints
  - Visa approval and entry permit issuance
  - Visa stamping in passport (can be done in UAE or at UAE embassy abroad)
  - Emirates ID card collection (delivered within 2 weeks of approval)
- **Tax residency certificate (if client requests, 2-4 weeks):**
  - Register with Federal Tax Authority (FTA)
  - Submit residency evidence: visa, Emirates ID, utility bills, tenancy contract or title deed
  - Obtain UAE Tax Residency Certificate for treaty benefits in home country

**What partner does:**
- Coordinate with WTP on company structure (if applicable): share allocation, director appointment, confirm tax treatment
- Advise client on tax residency strategy: does client need 183 days in UAE for home country deregistration?
- Review tax residency certificate for use in client's home jurisdiction tax filings

**Timeline:** 6-10 weeks (company + banking + visa overlapping, not sequential)

**Deliverables:**
- WTP: Company formation documents (if applicable), bank account confirmation letter and cards, visa stamps in passport, Emirates ID, tax residency certificate (if requested)
- Partner: Coordination notes on tax residency use in home jurisdiction

## Phase 6: Post-Purchase (Property Management & Ongoing Compliance)

**Objective:** Ensure property is operationally sound, income-generating (if investment), and compliance maintained.

**What WTP does:**
- **Snagging and handover (1-2 weeks after key collection):**
  - Professional snagging inspection: defect list (paint, fixtures, appliances, MEP)
  - Developer coordination: submit snagging report, track rectification
  - Final handover sign-off once defects resolved
- **Property management setup (if rental investment):**
  - RERA registration: register property with Dubai Real Estate Regulatory Agency
  - Tenant search: marketing, viewings, tenant screening
  - Tenancy contract drafting and EJARI registration (mandatory Dubai tenancy registration)
  - Rent collection and remittance to client account
  - Maintenance coordination: AC servicing, plumbing, community fee payments
- **Ongoing compliance (annual/as needed):**
  - Visa renewals: Golden Visa renewal after 10 years, employment visa renewals annually if applicable
  - Company renewals: license renewal, audit (if applicable), ESR filing (Economic Substance Regulations)
  - Tax filings: corporate tax return (if company structure), VAT returns (if applicable)
  - Banking relationship maintenance: update KYC docs annually, coordinate with bank on any compliance requests
  - Property management reporting: quarterly financial reports (rental income, expenses, net yield)

**What partner does:**
- Remain client's primary advisor for tax and wealth planning
- Coordinate with WTP on any major decisions: property sale, structure changes, additional property purchases
- Annual tax planning review: use WTP-provided UAE documents (tax residency cert, bank statements, visa copy) for client's home jurisdiction filings

**Timeline:** Ongoing (retainer-based relationship)

**Deliverables:**
- WTP: Snagging report, RERA registration certificate, tenancy contracts, quarterly property management reports, annual compliance summary (visa/license/tax status)
- Partner: Annual coordination call with WTP and client to review UAE setup and plan next steps

## Key Success Factors

**For WTP:**
- Execute Phase 2 (pre-screen) with rigor — no false positives on banking
- Deliver weekly status updates during Phase 4-5 (transaction and infrastructure)
- Maintain transparency: if delays occur (bank requests more docs, visa processing slows), communicate immediately

**For Partner:**
- Provide full client context at Phase 1 (referral) — the more WTP knows upfront, the better pre-screen accuracy
- Coordinate structure decisions (Phase 3-4) — personal vs. company ownership has tax implications, partner must advise
- Stay engaged during Phase 5-6 — client should see partner and WTP as integrated team, not separate vendors

**For Client (communicated by Partner):**
- Understand that Phase 2 is the decision gate — if banking is Red, property purchase is not advisable
- Expect 3-4 month end-to-end timeline from referral to "keys + visa + bank account in hand"
- Commit to UAE substance if tax residency is the goal (183 days/year, utility bills, real presence)
