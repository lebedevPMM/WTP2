# Golden Visa Through Property — Quick Guide for Partners

**The conversation your clients are already starting. Here's how to lead it.**

Every second UK and German HNWI is asking about UAE residency right now — driven by non-dom abolition (UK, April 2025), Wegzugsteuer pressure (DE), and Box 3 changes (NL, 2026). Most advisors react. This guide puts you ahead.

## Section 1: Eligibility

**Property value threshold:** 2,000,000 AED (~$545,000 USD) minimum.

**Eligible property types:**
- Completed residential property (ready, not off-plan)
- Off-plan property if purchased from approved developers and at least 50% paid
- Land with construction project approval
- Property purchased through mortgage (value = purchase price, not equity)

**Timeline:**
- Application submission: within 6 months of property registration
- Processing: 6-8 weeks from complete document submission
- Visa validity: 10 years, renewable

**Dependents included:**
- Spouse
- Unmarried children (no age limit if financially dependent or studying)
- Parents (if main applicant has minimum 15,000 AED/month income)

**Key requirement:** Property must be retained throughout visa validity. Sale triggers visa cancellation unless replaced with equivalent property.

## Section 2: The Process (Step-by-Step)

**Week 0-2: Property selection and pre-screen**
- WTP conducts banking eligibility check (critical: can client open UAE bank account?)
- Property selection aligned to Golden Visa requirements
- Structure advisory: personal ownership vs. company ownership (tax implications vary by country)

**Week 2-3: Property purchase**
- Sale and Purchase Agreement (SPA) signed
- Deposit transferred to escrow (typically 10%)
- Mortgage pre-approval if applicable

**Week 3-4: Transaction close**
- Full payment, DLD (Dubai Land Department) registration
- Title deed issued
- For off-plan: Oqood certificate (interim registration)

**Week 4-6: Visa application**
- Document preparation: title deed, passport copies, Emirates ID application, proof of relationship for dependents
- Medical fitness test and Emirates ID biometrics
- Application submission through approved channels

**Week 6-8: Visa issuance**
- Approval received
- Entry permit issued
- Visa stamping in passport
- Emirates ID card delivery (within 2 weeks)

**Total timeline:** 6-8 weeks from property purchase to visa in hand (assuming no complications).

## Section 3: Common Mistakes

**Mistake 1: Buying before bank pre-check**
- Problem: Client purchases 2M AED property, then discovers no bank will open account for SOF reasons.
- Result: Property owner without banking infrastructure, cannot transfer funds in/out efficiently.
- WTP approach: Banking pre-screen BEFORE property search.

**Mistake 2: Wrong ownership structure**
- Problem: UK client buys in personal name, triggers UK tax reporting. German client structures through offshore company, creates Wegzugsteuer complications.
- Result: Unintended tax exposure, compliance burden, potential penalties.
- WTP approach: Structure advisory based on client's home jurisdiction tax rules.

**Mistake 3: Insufficient SOF documentation**
- Problem: Client has purchase funds but cannot document clean source trail.
- Result: Visa approved but banking rejected, or prolonged account opening (6+ months).
- WTP approach: SOF assessment during pre-screen, document preparation roadmap before transaction.

**Mistake 4: Choosing non-eligible off-plan**
- Problem: Not all developers qualify for Golden Visa via off-plan route.
- Result: Client pays 50%+, discovers property doesn't qualify, must wait for completion (2-3 years).
- WTP approach: Developer eligibility verification before SPA.

**Mistake 5: Ignoring exit tax in home country**
- Problem: DE client relocates to UAE via Golden Visa but doesn't structure exit properly, triggers 30-40% Wegzugsteuer on unrealized gains.
- Result: UAE move costs more than tax saved.
- WTP approach: Partner coordination — you handle exit tax, we handle UAE entry structure.

**Mistake 6: Buying property "too cheap" for banking**
- Problem: 2M AED property in secondary market area doesn't signal wealth to UAE banks (they prefer 3M+ AED or prime locations).
- Result: Account opening delays, multiple rejections.
- WTP approach: Banking scenario modeling — if SOF requires premium profile, we recommend 3M+ AED property in prime area.

## Section 4: Tax Implications by Country

**UK (Non-Dom Changes)**
- From April 2025: Non-dom regime abolished, replaced with 4-year FIG (Foreign Income and Gains) regime.
- UAE Golden Visa establishes tax residency in UAE (if 183+ days/year physically present).
- Double Tax Treaty UK-UAE: rental income from UK property taxed in UK, can be exempt in UAE if structured correctly.
- CGT on UK property: payable even if UAE tax resident. Exit planning critical before relocation.
- Advisory need: Coordinate with UK tax advisor on timing of relocation, CGT structuring, trust unwinding.

**Germany (Wegzugsteuer — Exit Tax)**
- Exit tax triggered: if shareholding >1% in any company and relocation outside EU/EEA.
- Tax: 25% on unrealized gains at departure (can be deferred if move to EU, not UAE).
- Golden Visa does NOT exempt from Wegzugsteuer.
- Structuring: Must handle BEFORE move. Options: realize gains before exit, corporate restructuring, or accept payment.
- Advisory need: Exit tax calculation, restructuring options, timing of property purchase relative to exit.

**Netherlands (Box 3 Changes)**
- From 2026: Box 3 shifts to actual returns (vs. deemed return), but high effective tax on savings/investments.
- UAE tax residency: Requires 183+ days in UAE + deregistration from BRP (Dutch municipal register).
- Golden Visa route: Establish UAE residency, demonstrate center of life shift, apply for Dutch non-residency.
- Property structuring: Personal vs. BV (Dutch company) ownership has different tax treatment on exit.
- Advisory need: Dutch deregistration process, Box 3 exit planning, treaty benefits.

**Switzerland**
- Varies by canton. Wealth tax burden drives relocation.
- Golden Visa: Establishes UAE residency for treaty purposes.
- Exit: Cantonal exit tax on departure (if applicable), coordination with Swiss advisor critical.
- Banking: Swiss clients often retain Swiss accounts — WTP coordinates UAE account as secondary, not primary.

**General principle across jurisdictions:**
- Golden Visa = tool for UAE residency, NOT automatic tax optimization.
- Each country has specific exit rules — partner must coordinate exit, WTP coordinates entry.
- Best outcome: Partner handles home country exit structuring, WTP handles UAE banking + visa + property + substance setup.
