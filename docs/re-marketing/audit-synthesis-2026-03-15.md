## Top 5 Priority Fixes (Cross-Channel)

### 1. FIX GOOGLE ADS CHARACTER COUNTS (Launch Blocker)
**Impact:** CRITICAL — ads will be rejected at upload
**Action:** Re-count every description across all 27 RSAs. Hard limit: 90 characters. Many currently at 120-150 chars. Also verify all headlines ≤30 chars.
**Owner:** Ads team
**Timeline:** Before any Google Ads launch

### 2. BUILD CRM PIPELINE (Foundation)
**Impact:** HIGH — without this, all channels generate leads that decay
**Action:** Define: tool (Bitrix24 per strategy), pipeline stages (Contact → Qualified → Meeting → Pilot Case → Deal), required fields per contact (name, company, role, country, source channel, interest level, budget range), automation rules (source tagging, follow-up reminders)
**Owner:** Operations
**Timeline:** Month 1 (before paid channels launch)

### 3. CREATE LEAD CAPTURE FUNNEL (Conversion Infrastructure)
**Impact:** HIGH — all CTAs currently lead to incomplete paths
**Action:** Build: (a) Link-in-bio landing page (3-5 links: Pre-Screen, Golden Visa Guide, RAK Report, Book Call, WhatsApp), (b) Pre-screen intake form (3 fields for launch: Name, Email, Phone), (c) Email nurture sequence (3 emails over 7 days), (d) WhatsApp auto-reply with pre-screen questionnaire, (e) UTM tracking for attribution
**Owner:** Marketing + Dev
**Timeline:** Month 1

### 4. ADD PROOF POINTS (Credibility)
**Impact:** MEDIUM-HIGH — assertions without evidence erode trust
**Action:** (a) Source or soften "40% of buyers face banking problems" statistic, (b) Create 2-3 anonymized partner case studies ("UK tax advisor, 3 referrals in Q4, $X earned"), (c) Verify "300+ cases" and "94% approval rate" with WTP operations, (d) Replace unverifiable numbers with softer language if needed
**Owner:** Content + Operations
**Timeline:** Before distributing partner materials

### 5. COMPLETE DE/NL CONTENT (Market Expansion)
**Impact:** MEDIUM-HIGH — unlocks 40%+ of target partner base
**Action:** (a) Full webinar script for Germany Exit Tax (Wegzugsteuer), (b) New webinar for Netherlands Box 3, (c) German/Dutch language Google Ads variants (lower CPC opportunity), (d) DE/NL-specific LinkedIn posts (2-3 per month)
**Owner:** Content
**Timeline:** Month 2-3

## Per-Channel Detailed Audit Summaries

### Partners (8.5/10)

**Docs reviewed:** RE-One-Pager-EN.md, RE-One-Pager-RU.md, Golden-Visa-Quick-Guide.md, RE-Service-Map.md

**Highlights:**
- One-Pager hook is textbook pain-point copywriting ("Client signed 3M AED. Bank declined account")
- "What We Won't Do" section = Pratfall Effect (admitting limitations builds trust)
- Referral Fee Schedule (20-35%) with industry comparison = Contrast Effect + Anchoring
- "Start With One Case" CTA = zero activation energy (Foot-in-the-Door)
- Golden Visa Guide Section 4 (tax by country) = crown jewel — speaks partner's language

**Issues:**
- Timeline: One-Pager says 48h, Service Map says 5-7 days — resolve before distribution
- No partner testimonials in any document
- Golden Visa "Quick Guide" is 234 lines — not quick. Rename or create actual 2-pager
- Service Map reads like internal SOP, not partner-facing document
- Missing: visual service map flowchart, referral agreement template, FAQ document

### LinkedIn (8/10)

**Docs reviewed:** 4-week-content-plan.md, post-drafts.md, newsletter-template.md

**Highlights:**
- Branded frameworks (#BankingFirstMethod, 3-Question Filter) are strong IP
- Hook quality excellent across all 12 posts
- Content funnel balanced: 25% awareness / 55% education / 20% conversion
- Newsletter WTP Take section with internal data (12 pre-screens, 33% failure) = insider value

**Issues:**
- 4/12 core posts (33%) have no banking angle — Banking-First should be the lens for ALL content
- Personal brand (Konstantin PMM Consulting) vs WTP RE content = identity conflict on LinkedIn
- Newsletter positioned as market intelligence, not banking-informed intelligence
- Zero video content in plan
- No competitor content analysis / outlier research done
- No engagement target list (who are the 5-10 profiles to comment on?)

### Instagram/YouTube (8/10)

**Docs reviewed:** content-plan-month1.md, reel-scripts.md, youtube-scripts.md

**Highlights:**
- Reel scripts are production-ready with scene-by-scene direction
- YouTube scripts are broadcast-quality with B-roll inserts and timing markers
- Banking-First woven into every script without feeling forced
- Cross-platform repurposing flow well-diagrammed

**Issues:**
- No paid amplification — new account organic reach is near-zero. Need $200-500/mo for Reel boosts
- No geo-targeting for EU audience (posting times UAE-optimized, target is UK/DE/NL)
- Hashtags are all high-competition (#DubaiRealEstate 1M+ posts). Need mid-tail rotation
- No YouTube Shorts strategy (fastest growth lever for new channels)
- No lead capture funnel spec (all CTAs = "link in bio" but no funnel defined)
- Video 1 at 15 min is too long for <1,000 subscriber channel (optimal: 8-12 min)

### Google Ads (8.5/10)

**Docs reviewed:** campaign-setup.md, ad-copy-library.md, landing-page-spec.md

**Highlights:**
- Campaign 2 (banking/structure intent) = unique niche no competitor bids on
- 27 RSAs + 5 banner designs + 2 YouTube scripts = complete creative library
- Dynamic headline matching by utm_campaign on LP = 30-50% conversion lift potential
- Comparison table ("8,000 Agents — What Makes WTP Different?") = strongest LP element
- Progressive budget rollout recommended (don't launch all 4 campaigns at once)

**Issues:**
- **CRITICAL:** Many descriptions exceed 90-char limit (up to 147 chars). Ads WILL be rejected.
- 6 form fields = too many for cold Google Ads traffic. Start with 3, add qualification in follow-up
- $1,600-2,500/month across 4 campaigns / 9 ad groups is too thin. Focus: Campaign 1+2 only for months 1-3
- Blog pages referenced but not created (4 articles needed before Campaigns 2-3 launch)
- Google Optimize referenced but deprecated (use VWO/Optimizely)
- No Thank You page spec (conversion tracking, next steps)

### Portal Listings (8/10)

**Docs reviewed:** listing-templates.md, agent-profiles.md

**Highlights:**
- 8 listing templates covering full segment range (Golden Visa, luxury, villa, off-plan, RAK, Expo City, commercial, ultra-luxury)
- Banking pre-screen is FIRST advisory item in every template
- Words to Use / Words to Avoid list = brand voice enforcement
- Corporate Integration Specialist profile template = unique in market

**Issues:**
- Portal SEO guidance is surface-level (4 lines). Need: algorithm ranking factors, field mapping, refresh cadence
- No Bayut/PF CMS field mapping tables (great copy + empty fields = invisible in filtered searches)
- No lead-to-Service-Map handoff protocol (what happens after portal inquiry?)
- No competitor listing analysis
- No multilingual listing strategy (Russian/Arabic variants needed)

### Events (8/10)

**Docs reviewed:** investor-dinner-pack.md, webinar-template.md, conference-checklist.md

**Highlights:**
- Investor dinner pack = 9/10. Banking-First is the structural climax (Slides 8-9)
- "This is NOT a sales pitch" framing = Pratfall + Reciprocity + Contrast
- Webinar #1 (UK Non-Doms) has full production-ready script, time-coded
- "Pilot case" CTA = perfect B2B conversion chain (Foot-in-the-Door → Commitment)
- Competitor intelligence section in conference checklist = unique

**Issues:**
- No digital lead capture at events (QR code, iPad form). Only business cards
- Webinars #2-4 are outlines, not full scripts (3/4 not production-ready)
- No NL webinar at all despite Netherlands being Tier 2 target
- Elevator pitch leads with company intro, not Banking-First hook
- No CRM integration guidance in any document
- Roadshow frequency too aggressive (16 trips/year; start with London only)
