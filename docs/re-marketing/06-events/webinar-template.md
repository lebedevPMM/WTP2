**Slide 6: Question 1 — Personal or Company Ownership?**
- Table: Personal vs. Company comparison

| Factor | Personal Ownership | Company Ownership |
|--------|-------------------|------------------|
| **Golden Visa** | ✅ Eligible (2M+ AED) | ❌ Not eligible (company-owned ≠ personal) |
| **Tax efficiency (UK exit)** | Depends on deemed disposal | Can structure for lower exit tax |
| **IHT protection** | Limited | Stronger (non-UK structure) |
| **Mortgage availability** | Easier | Limited |
| **Flexibility** | Lower | Higher (sell shares, not property) |

**Script:**

"First question: should the property be in their personal name or a company?

**Personal ownership:**
- Pros: Golden Visa eligible, easier mortgage
- Cons: Less tax flexibility on UK exit, IHT exposure

**Company ownership (UAE or offshore LLC):**
- Pros: Better UK exit tax structuring, IHT mitigation, flexibility
- Cons: No Golden Visa from company-owned property, harder financing

**Our default for UK clients:**
- If Golden Visa is critical → personal ownership + separate UAE company for substance
- If tax structuring is critical → company ownership + alternative visa route (investor visa via company)

You as the tax advisor decide. We execute.

**Slide 8: Question 3 — UK Exit Tax Liability**
- Headline: "Exit Tax Isn't Eliminated — It's Triggered Early"
- Diagram: UK Exit → Deemed Disposal → CGT + Inheritance Tax Start

**Script:**

"Third question: what's their UK exit tax liability?

Leaving the UK doesn't erase tax — it accelerates it.

**Deemed disposal:**
- Certain assets treated as sold when you leave
- Capital gains tax triggered on exit (not when actually sold)
- Can be 10-28% depending on asset type

**Inheritance tax:**
- UK assets remain in IHT scope for 3-5 years after exit (depending on domicile status)
- Worldwide assets may still be caught if domicile not properly broken

**Your role as tax advisor:** Calculate exit liability, plan timing, structure assets pre-exit.

**Our role:** Execute the UAE side — company, visa, property, banking — so your client has clean UAE tax residency when they exit UK.

**This is a partnership.** You handle UK exit. We handle UAE entry.

## Tips for Success

### DO:
✅ Start on time (respect attendees' calendars)
✅ Use real case examples (anonymized but detailed)
✅ Encourage questions throughout (not just at end)
✅ Offer something valuable for free (pilot case, guide, report)
✅ Follow up within 24 hours (while memory is fresh)
✅ Repurpose recording into clips for social media

### DON'T:
❌ Run over time (45 min max — people drop off after)
❌ Pitch hard (education first, partnership offer second)
❌ Ignore chat/Q&A (assign someone to monitor)
❌ Use jargon without explanation (audience may be new to UAE)
❌ Skip follow-up (80% of value is in follow-up nurture)
❌ Forget to record (recording = evergreen asset)
