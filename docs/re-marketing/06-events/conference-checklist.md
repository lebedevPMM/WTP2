# Conference Checklist & Roadshow Playbook

> Complete guide for attending RE conferences and executing 1-day partner roadshows
> Two strategies: visitor (no booth) and exhibitor (with booth)

**Target Conferences:** Cityscape Dubai, MIPIM Cannes, IFN Forum, Wealth Forum
**Roadshow Cities:** London, Frankfurt, Munich, Amsterdam, Zurich
**Investment per Conference:** $1,000-10,000 (visitor vs. exhibitor)
**ROI Target:** 15-20 quality contacts per event, 3-5 partnerships per year

## Visitor Strategy (No Booth)

**When to use:** First-time attending, budget-conscious, testing conference ROI
**Investment:** $500-3,000 (ticket + travel + materials)
**Goal:** 15-20 quality contacts, 3-5 follow-up meetings scheduled

### Pre-Conference Preparation (3 Weeks Before)

#### Week 1-2: Research & Outreach

- [ ] **Download attendee list** (if available) — Most conferences offer exhibitor/speaker lists
- [ ] **Identify targets** — 30-40 people to connect with:
  - Speakers on relevant topics (RE investment, tax, wealth management)
  - Exhibitors from target countries (UK, DE, NL, CH)
  - Wealth managers, family offices, tax advisory firms
- [ ] **LinkedIn outreach** — Message 20-30 targets:

**Template:**
"Hi [Name], saw you're speaking at [Conference] next week. I'm attending from Dubai (WTP — we work with UK/DE advisors on UAE RE). Would love to connect briefly at the event to discuss [specific topic]. Coffee on [Day 1] or [Day 2]?"

- [ ] **Schedule meetings** — Aim for 3-5 pre-scheduled meetings during conference
- [ ] **Book accommodation** — Hotel near venue, 1-2 nights depending on conference length

#### Week 3: Materials & Logistics

- [ ] **Print business cards** — 100 cards (expect to hand out 30-50, rest = backup)
- [ ] **Print One-Pagers** — 50 copies, tri-fold or A4 (WTP RE One-Pager)
- [ ] **Create QR code** — Links to:
  - realestate.wtpref.com
  - Contact form
  - LinkedIn profile
- [ ] **Prepare elevator pitch** — 30-second version:

**Example:**
"I'm [Name] from WTP in Dubai. We work with UK and European tax advisors whose clients are buying property in UAE — but we take a banking-first approach. Most buyers struggle with UAE bank accounts AFTER purchase. We pre-screen banking before property search. Saves headaches, speeds up the process."

- [ ] **Prepare conversation starters** — 3-5 open-ended questions:
  - "What brings you to [Conference]?"
  - "Are you seeing clients interested in UAE property?"
  - "What's the biggest challenge your clients face with cross-border investments?"
- [ ] **Download conference app** — Most events have mobile apps with schedule, attendee directory, messaging
- [ ] **Plan daily schedule** — Mark must-attend sessions, networking breaks, lunch/dinner slots

### Day-Of Strategy

#### Morning (Before Sessions)

- [ ] **Arrive early** — 8:00-8:30 for 9:00 start (networking during registration)
- [ ] **Position near registration** — Strike up conversations while people collect badges
- [ ] **Scan badges** — If conference provides badge scanners, rent one ($50-100) — faster than collecting cards

#### During Sessions

- [ ] **Attend relevant sessions** — Pick 2-3 where your ICP will be
  - Sessions on wealth management, tax planning, international RE
  - NOT generic sessions (less targeted audience)
- [ ] **Sit strategically** — Aisle or back row (easy to leave for networking breaks)
- [ ] **Take notes** — On content AND on speakers (potential contacts)
- [ ] **Ask questions** — During Q&A (positions you as expert, people approach you after)

#### Networking Breaks (Critical)

- [ ] **Don't sit alone** — Approach groups of 2-3 people (easier than large groups)
- [ ] **Use conference context** — "What did you think of [Speaker]'s point about [topic]?"
- [ ] **Exchange cards** — After 2-3 min conversation, offer card
- [ ] **Note on card** — Immediately after conversation, write on BACK of their card:
  - Where you met (session name)
  - Key point discussed
  - Follow-up action (e.g., "Send RAK report")
- [ ] **Quality over quantity** — 5 deep conversations > 20 shallow ones

#### Lunch & Dinner

- [ ] **Sit with strangers** — Don't default to people you know
- [ ] **Introduce yourself to table** — "Hi everyone, I'm [Name], WTP from Dubai. What brings you all here?"
- [ ] **Share insights** — If Dubai RE comes up, offer data/perspective (not pitch)
- [ ] **Collect contacts** — Aim for 3-5 new contacts per meal

#### End of Day

- [ ] **Follow up same evening** — LinkedIn connect + short message:

**Template:**
"Hi [Name], great meeting you at [Conference] today. Really interesting point you made about [topic]. Would love to continue the conversation — I'll send you our RAK market report. Stay in touch."

- [ ] **CRM update** — Log all new contacts, tag with conference name + interest level (hot/warm/cold)

### Content Harvesting at Conference

Every conference = 10-15 content pieces. Capture as you go:

| Asset | How to Capture | Content Use |
|-------|---------------|-------------|
| Speaker quotes | Photo of slide + note key phrase | LinkedIn text post |
| Your Q&A moment | Ask someone to record when you ask a question | LinkedIn video clip |
| Hallway conversations | Selfie with permission | LinkedIn "Met [Name] at [Conference]" |
| Venue/crowd shots | Phone photos | Instagram Stories, LinkedIn |
| Key data from presentations | Photo of slides | LinkedIn carousel (3-5 data points) |
| Your insights from sessions | Voice memo between sessions | LinkedIn post next day |

**Rule:** Capture raw assets DURING conference. Polish and post AFTER (or same evening).

### Social Media During Conference

- [ ] **3-5 posts per day** (LinkedIn + Instagram):
  - Morning: "At [Conference] today — excited to hear [Speaker] on [topic]"
  - Midday: Photo from session or networking break + insight
  - Evening: "Day 1 wrap: Key takeaway from [Conference]..."

- [ ] **Tag speakers and organizers** — Increases visibility
- [ ] **Use conference hashtag** — Most events have official hashtag
- [ ] **Go live (optional)** — Short Instagram/LinkedIn live: "Live from [Conference]" (1-2 min)

### Post-Conference Follow-Up (Within 48 Hours)

- [ ] **LinkedIn connects** — Send to ALL new contacts (same day or next morning)
- [ ] **Email follow-ups** — Segment by conversation:

**Segment 1: Hot Leads (3-5 people)**
Subject: "Following up from [Conference] — [specific topic]"
Body: Personal, references specific conversation, offers next step (call, report, pilot case)

**Segment 2: Warm Contacts (10-15 people)**
Subject: "Great meeting you at [Conference]"
Body: Brief reminder of conversation + useful resource (market report, guide)

**Segment 3: Cold Contacts (rest)**
Subject: "Connecting from [Conference]"
Body: Generic but friendly, add to newsletter list

- [ ] **Schedule calls** — Book 3-5 follow-up calls with hot leads (within 2 weeks)
- [ ] **Send promised materials** — If you said "I'll send you X," do it within 24 hours
- [ ] **Update CRM** — Full notes on each contact, next action, timeline

## Booth Strategy (Cityscape Dubai)

**When to use:** Home market conference, established presence, higher budget
**Investment:** $5,000-10,000 (booth + design + materials + staffing)
**Goal:** 50-100 contacts, 10-15 qualified leads, brand visibility

### 3 Months Before: Booth Booking

- [ ] **Book booth space** — Early bird pricing (save 20-30%)
- [ ] **Choose location** — Priority:
  1. Near main entrance or registration
  2. Near food court / coffee area (high foot traffic)
  3. Corner booth (two open sides = more visibility)

### 6 Weeks Before: Booth Design

- [ ] **Design brief** — Share with designer:

**Elements:**
- Roll-up banners (2x, 200cm tall): WTP logo + "Banking-First Real Estate" + key stat (e.g., "Pre-Screen Banking in 5-7 Days")
- Table cover with WTP branding
- iPad mount or tablet stand (for landing page demo)
- Brochure holder (for One-Pagers)
- Business card holder
- Small giveaway (optional): Branded notebooks, pens, USB drives

**Design principles:**
- Clean, professional, not cluttered
- Large text (readable from 3-5 meters)
- Contrast colors (dark background, light text or vice versa)
- Clear CTA: "Free Banking Pre-Screen — Scan QR Code"

- [ ] **Print materials:**
  - 2 roll-up banners (print in Dubai, cheaper than international shipping)
  - 200 One-Pagers (tri-fold or A4)
  - 200 business cards (split between 2 staff members)
  - 50 Market Reports (premium leave-behind for qualified leads)

### 2 Weeks Before: Logistics

- [ ] **Confirm booth setup time** — Usually day before conference opens
- [ ] **Staffing plan** — 2 people minimum:
  - Person A: On booth (greet, qualify, capture leads)
  - Person B: Floor networking (walk around, bring people back to booth)
  - Shifts: 3-hour rotations (avoid fatigue)

- [ ] **Lead capture system** — Choose one:
  - **Option 1:** Badge scanner (rent from conference, $100-200)
  - **Option 2:** iPad with form (Google Form / Typeform / Airtable)
  - **Option 3:** Business cards + manual CRM entry (slowest)

- [ ] **Prepare conversation scripts** (for booth staff):

**Opener (as people walk by):**
"Hi, are you investing in Dubai property or advising clients who are?"

**If YES:**
"Great — have you heard about the banking-first approach? Most buyers struggle to open UAE bank accounts AFTER purchasing. We reverse that. Quick overview?" [Hand them One-Pager]

**If NO:**
"No worries — here's our contact info if it comes up." [Hand business card, let them go]

**Qualifier questions (for serious leads):**
1. What's your timeline for property purchase?
2. What's your budget range?
3. Have you considered banking and visa requirements?
4. Are you working with any advisors (tax, wealth manager)?

**Lead capture:**
"Let me grab your contact — I'll send you [market report / pre-screen info / specific resource]. What's your email?"

### Day-Of: Booth Operations

#### Setup (Day Before or Morning Of)

- [ ] **Arrive 2 hours early** — Set up booth, test tech
- [ ] **Arrange materials** — One-Pagers front and center, Market Reports behind counter (hand out selectively)
- [ ] **Test iPad/scanner** — Make sure forms work, internet connection stable
- [ ] **Booth appearance** — Clean, organized, inviting (no clutter, no empty spaces)

#### During Conference

- [ ] **Stand, don't sit** — Sitting signals "not interested in talking"
- [ ] **Greet proactively** — Don't wait for people to approach, engage eye contact
- [ ] **Qualify fast** — Don't spend 20 min with tire-kickers, politely exit and move to next
- [ ] **Capture leads immediately** — Don't trust memory, log contact while talking or right after
- [ ] **Rotate staff** — Switch booth/floor roles every 2-3 hours
- [ ] **Replenish materials** — Check hourly, restock One-Pagers and cards

#### Floor Networking (Person B)

- [ ] **Attend 1-2 sessions** — Choose high-value sessions, network during breaks
- [ ] **Bring people to booth** — "Let me show you our booth, it's right around the corner"
- [ ] **Partner booth visits** — Visit complementary booths (tax firms, immigration consultants) — introduce yourself, discuss partnership
- [ ] **Collect competitor intel** — What are other RE agencies offering? Pricing? Messaging?

### Post-Conference (Within 24 Hours)

- [ ] **Export leads** — Download from scanner or form, import to CRM
- [ ] **Segment leads:**
  - **Hot** (ready to buy, timeline <3 months)
  - **Warm** (exploring, timeline 3-6 months)
  - **Cold** (information gathering, timeline >6 months or unclear)

- [ ] **Email follow-up:**

**Hot Leads (within 12 hours):**
Subject: "Following up from Cityscape — [Name]"
Body: Personal, reference specific conversation, offer next step (call this week, send property options, pre-screen)

**Warm Leads (within 24 hours):**
Subject: "Great meeting you at Cityscape"
Body: Brief reminder + Market Report PDF attached

**Cold Leads (within 48 hours):**
Subject: "Connecting from Cityscape Dubai"
Body: Thanks for stopping by + add to newsletter

- [ ] **Schedule calls** — Book 5-10 calls with hot/warm leads (within 1 week)
- [ ] **ROI analysis:**
  - Leads captured: [X]
  - Hot leads: [Y]
  - Calls scheduled: [Z]
  - Cost per lead: [booth cost ÷ total leads]
  - Projected revenue: [hot leads × avg deal size × close rate]

## Road-Show Playbook (1-Day Partner Visit)

**Format:** 1 day in London/Frankfurt/Amsterdam — 2-3 partner meetings + evening mini-dinner
**Investment:** $1,500-2,500 (flight + hotel + dinner + materials)
**Goal:** Activate 2-3 partners, generate 1-2 referrals within 30 days

### When to Do Roadshows

- **Trigger 1:** You have 5-10 partner contacts in one city (worth consolidating into 1 trip)
- **Trigger 2:** One partner in city is "hot" (ready to refer) — use trip to activate 2-3 others
- **Frequency:** 1 roadshow per quarter per city (London Q1, Frankfurt Q2, Amsterdam Q3, Zurich Q4)

### Cities & Audience

| City | Primary Audience | Best Timing | Notes |
|------|-----------------|-------------|-------|
| **London** | UK tax advisors, wealth managers, family offices | Q1 (tax year start), Q4 (non-dom planning) | Largest market, most non-dom relocators |
| **Frankfurt** | German tax advisors, Steuerberater, corporate advisors | Q2, Q3 (avoid Dec-Feb tax season) | Exit tax specialists, corporate relocations |
| **Munich** | Same as Frankfurt + entrepreneurs | Q2, Q3 | Alternative to Frankfurt, strong entrepreneur base |
| **Amsterdam** | Dutch wealth managers, tax advisors | Q2, Q4 | Box 3 changes driving interest |
| **Zurich** | Swiss family offices, private bankers | Q1, Q3 | High-net-worth, multi-generational wealth |

### 3 Weeks Before: Preparation

#### Week 1-2: Outreach & Scheduling

- [ ] **List target partners** — 8-10 contacts in city:
  - 3-5 existing contacts (warm)
  - 3-5 cold prospects (LinkedIn, referrals)

- [ ] **Send meeting requests:**

**Template (Existing Contact):**
"Hi [Name], I'll be in [City] on [Date] — would love to catch up in person. Are you free for coffee at [Time]? I can come to your office or meet nearby."

**Template (Cold Prospect):**
"Hi [Name], I'm visiting [City] next month to meet with advisors working with UAE-bound clients. Saw your profile — would a 30-min coffee make sense? Happy to share what we're seeing with UK/DE clients relocating to Dubai."

- [ ] **Aim to schedule:**
  - 10:00 — Meeting 1 (existing partner)
  - 12:30 — Lunch meeting (new prospect)
  - 15:00 — Meeting 2 (new prospect)
  - 19:00 — Dinner (5-8 invitees — mini investor dinner format)

#### Week 3: Logistics

- [ ] **Book flights** — Arrive evening before, leave next evening (1 night stay)
- [ ] **Book hotel** — Central location, walkable to meetings
- [ ] **Book dinner venue** — Private dining room, 6-10 person capacity
  - London: DIFC-equivalent (Mayfair, City area)
  - Frankfurt: Westend or Altstadt
  - Amsterdam: Grachtengordel (canal ring) or Zuidas

- [ ] **Print materials:**
  - 20 One-Pagers
  - 20 business cards
  - 10 Market Reports (for dinner attendees)

- [ ] **Prepare materials (digital):**
  - Presentation deck (PDF on iPad) — 10-slide version of investor dinner deck
  - Partnership agreement template (if partner ready to sign)

### Day-Of: Roadshow Schedule

#### 10:00 — Meeting 1 (Existing Partner)

**Location:** Their office or nearby café
**Duration:** 45-60 min
**Goal:** Deepen relationship, discuss pipeline, ask for referrals

**Agenda:**
1. Catch up (5 min) — How's business, any updates since last call
2. Discuss current pipeline (10 min) — Any clients in their funnel who fit UAE profile
3. Share market update (15 min) — Show 3-5 slides (RAK opportunity, banking changes, Golden Visa updates)
4. Ask for referrals (10 min) — "Who else in your network should I meet while I'm here?"
5. Discuss co-marketing (10 min) — Joint webinar, co-branded guide, client event
6. Next steps (5 min) — Book follow-up call, set goals for next quarter

**Materials to bring:**
- Updated Market Report (hand them a print copy)
- Partnership performance (if they've referred clients): "You've sent us 3 clients, generated £X in fees"

#### 12:30 — Lunch Meeting (New Prospect)

**Location:** Restaurant near their office
**Duration:** 60-75 min
**Goal:** Establish relationship, understand their client base, pitch partnership

**Agenda:**
1. Introductions (10 min) — Their background, clients, services
2. WTP overview (10 min) — Not a pitch, just context: "We work with advisors like you..."

6. Next steps (5 min) — If yes: "Let's start with one pilot case." If no: "Stay in touch, happy to be a resource."

**Materials:**
- One-Pager (leave with them)
- iPad with presentation (if conversation goes deep)

**Location:** Their office or café
**Duration:** 45-60 min
**Goal:** Same as lunch meeting

**Repeat lunch meeting agenda.** Adjust based on learnings from lunch.

**Location:** Private dining room
**Attendees:** 5-8 people:
- 2-3 existing partners
- 2-3 new prospects (from today's meetings or other contacts)

**Format:** Scaled-down investor dinner

## Competitor Intelligence at Conferences

Conferences = rare opportunity to see competitors in person. Capture intelligence systematically.

### What to Observe

| Category | What to Look For | How to Capture |
|----------|-----------------|----------------|
| **Messaging** | Headline on booth, tagline, value prop | Photo of booth |
| **Materials** | Brochures, flyers, business cards | Collect them (visit booth) |
| **Pricing** | Any visible pricing, fee structure mentioned | Notes on phone |
| **Staff quality** | How they pitch, questions they ask, professionalism | Mental notes → journal |
| **Traffic** | How many people visit their booth, queue length | Observation |
| **Technology** | CRM used, lead capture method, digital tools | Ask casually |

### Competitor Booth Visit Script

Visit 3-5 competitor RE agency booths. Present yourself as an investor (not a competitor):

"Hi, I'm exploring Dubai property for investment. Can you tell me about your services?"

**Listen for:**
- Do they mention banking? (99% won't — that's your differentiator)
- How do they qualify leads?
- What properties/areas do they push?
- Do they offer structure advice?

**After visit, note:**
- What they do well (learn from it)
- What they miss (your opportunity)
- How WTP is different (refine your pitch)

### Post-Conference Competitor Report

After each conference, write a 1-page summary:
- Top 3 competitors present
- Their messaging vs. WTP messaging
- Gaps they're not filling (banking, structure, visa integration)
- Ideas to steal (formats, materials, tech)

Save to: `docs/re-marketing/competitor-intel/[conference]-[date].md`

## Final Tips: Events & Roadshows

### DO:
✅ Prepare obsessively — know your pitch, your materials, your schedule
✅ Follow up within 24 hours (while memory is fresh)
✅ Qualify leads fast (don't waste time on tire-kickers)
✅ Bring value first (market report, insight, free assessment) before asking for business
✅ Track everything in CRM (no contact left behind)
✅ Post on social media during event (visibility + FOMO for next event)

### DON'T:
❌ Wing it (lack of preparation shows)
❌ Delay follow-up (hot leads go cold after 48 hours)
❌ Pitch too hard (education and insight sell better than hard sell)
❌ Ignore small contacts (today's junior advisor = tomorrow's partner)
❌ Skip ROI tracking (measure or you can't improve)
❌ Burn out (events are exhausting — pace yourself, take breaks)
