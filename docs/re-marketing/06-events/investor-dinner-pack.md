## Event Overview

### Purpose & Philosophy

This is NOT a sales pitch evening. This is a market insight event where serious investors gather to see data, ask questions, and understand the Dubai RE opportunity through a banking-first lens.

**What makes WTP dinners different:**
- Banking-first perspective (unique in Dubai RE market)
- Data over opinions — charts, graphs, regulatory updates
- Small group (10-15) allows real conversation
- No hard sell — we offer insight and free pre-screen, decision is theirs

### Event Format

**Duration:** 18:30-21:00 (2.5 hours)
**Attendance:** 10-15 HNWI investors
**Venue:** Private dining room (not restaurant floor — separate space)
**Dress code:** Business casual

**Timeline:**
```
18:30-19:00 (30min)  Welcome drinks + networking
19:00-19:20 (20min)  Presentation: "Dubai RE Market: What Data Shows for 2026"
19:20-20:20 (60min)  Seated dinner — conversation continues at table
20:20-20:40 (20min)  Open Q&A + WTP approach
20:40-21:00 (20min)  Closing + 1-on-1 conversations
```

## Tips for Success

### DO:
✅ Keep presentation data-heavy, opinion-light
✅ Address the banking problem directly — it's your differentiator
✅ Encourage questions throughout (not just Q&A at end)
✅ Take notes during conversations (who said what — for follow-up)
✅ Offer the free pre-screen multiple times (no pressure, but make it clear)
✅ Get photographer to capture group photo (social proof for next event)
✅ Follow up FAST (within 12 hours while memory fresh)

### DON'T:
❌ Pitch hard — data and insight sell themselves
❌ Overstay — 21:00 hard stop, respect attendees' time
❌ Ignore dietary restrictions — ask in RSVP
❌ Talk only to "big fish" — every attendee matters, network effects
❌ Skip follow-up — 80% of value is in the follow-up sequence
❌ Make it about WTP — make it about THEM and their decision
❌ Promise results you can't deliver — honesty builds trust

## Next Dinner: Lessons to Apply

After first dinner, review:
1. **Which topics got most questions?** → Double down on those in next presentation
2. **Where did people tune out?** → Cut or shorten those slides
3. **What objections surfaced?** → Add slides addressing them
4. **Who didn't follow up?** → Why? (Wrong audience, wrong message, wrong CTA?)
5. **Conversion rate?** → If <20% request consultation, adjust invitation targeting

**Iterate and improve.** First dinner = learning. Third dinner = dialed in.
