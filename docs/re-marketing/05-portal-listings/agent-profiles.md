✅ **Substance Compliance** — Office, staff, economic presence for tax residency
✅ **Partner Network** — Collaboration with your existing advisors (lawyers, accountants, tax specialists)

**Specializations**

Luxury residential (2M+ AED), Golden Visa properties, RAK emerging markets, Expo City, commercial real estate for corporate clients, family villas (substance/relocation), multi-jurisdictional ownership structures.

Because buying property in Dubai is an investment decision, not just a lifestyle choice. We approach it as your integrated advisory partner, not a transactional agent.

### Response Time SLA

- [ ] **15 minutes** — business hours (9am-7pm UAE time) for WhatsApp/call
- [ ] **2 hours** — evenings (7pm-11pm) for urgent inquiries
- [ ] **Next business day** — nights and weekends (set auto-reply)
- [ ] **24/7 WhatsApp Business** with automated greeting + "I'll respond within [timeframe]"

### Review Strategy

**When to ask for reviews:**
- After successful property viewing (if client is impressed)
- After offer acceptance (before closing)
- 2 weeks after closing (once settled, emotions positive)
- After successful Golden Visa approval (major milestone)

**How to ask:**
```
Hi [Name], hope you're settling in well. If you're happy with the service we provided, would you consider leaving a brief review on [Bayut/Property Finder]? It helps other families/investors find us. Here's the link: [link]. Thank you!
```

**Don't ask:**
- Immediately after viewing (too early)
- If client is frustrated/unresponsive
- Before deal is fully closed (risk of negative review if something goes wrong)

**Incentivize (optional):**
- Small gift after review (coffee voucher, plant for new home, etc.) — NOT payment for review

## Additional Best Practices

### Portal SEO

- **Use keywords in bio:** Golden Visa, luxury real estate Dubai, investment property, RAK specialist, etc.
- **Listing titles:** Include area + property type + key feature (e.g., "2BR Downtown Dubai — Golden Visa Eligible")
- **Description keywords:** Sprinkle naturally (banking, visa, structure, investment, yield)

### Engagement Tactics

- **Update listings weekly** — portals reward active accounts with better visibility
- **Respond to EVERY inquiry** — even if not qualified ("Thank you for reaching out. This property is [status]. Can I help you find something similar?")
- **Featured listings** — invest in 2-3 featured slots for best properties (premium placement)
- **Portal ads** — test $300-500/month on Bayut TruCheck or Property Finder Featured Agent

### Analytics Tracking

- **Weekly:** Review views, leads, conversion rate per listing
- **Monthly:** CPL (cost per lead) from portal subscription fees
- **Quarterly:** Lead quality assessment (portal vs other channels)
- **Optimize:** Pause underperforming listings, boost high-performers, test new templates
