## Content Production Workflow

**Instagram Reels (each):**
1. Script (10 min)
2. Shoot (5-10 min: talking head or B-roll voiceover)
3. Edit in CapCut (15-20 min: cuts, subtitles, music)
4. Export + post (5 min)
**Total time per Reel:** 35-45 minutes

**YouTube Videos (each):**
1. Script (60-90 min)
2. Graphics prep in Canva (30-45 min)
3. Shoot (60-90 min: talking head + B-roll)
4. Edit in DaVinci Resolve or Final Cut (3-4 hours: cuts, B-roll, graphics, music, color)
5. Thumbnail design (20 min)
6. Upload + description + tags (15 min)
**Total time per video:** 6-8 hours

**Weekly time budget:**
- Instagram: 3 Reels × 40 min = 2 hours
- Stories: 10 min/day × 7 = 70 min
- YouTube: 1 video every 2 weeks = 3-4 hours/week averaged
**Total:** ~6 hours/week for Month 1 content production
