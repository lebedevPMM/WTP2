## Production Notes

**Shooting Tips:**

1. **Lighting:** Natural light (near window) or ring light for talking head. Avoid harsh overhead lights.

2. **Audio:** CRITICAL. Use external mic (Rode VideoMicro or DJI Mic). Phone mic is NOT enough for credibility.

3. **Framing:** Talking head = centered or rule-of-thirds. Leave headroom. Eye level camera (not looking down at phone).

4. **B-roll:** Shoot 2-3x more than you need. 10 seconds of usable footage might require 30 seconds of shooting.

5. **Consistency:** Same background/location for talking head scenes = brand consistency.

**Editing Tips:**

1. **Subtitles:** MANDATORY. 85% of Instagram videos watched without sound. Use CapCut auto-captions, then manually fix errors.

2. **Pacing:** Cut every 3-5 seconds. Static shots lose attention. Jump cuts are fine (expected in Reels).

3. **Music:** Use Instagram music library (for Instagram) or Epidemic Sound (for cross-platform). Keep volume at 20-30% (voiceover should dominate).

4. **Text overlays:** Bold sans-serif fonts (Montserrat, Poppins). High contrast (white text on dark background or vice versa). Readable on mobile.

5. **Hook:** First 3 seconds MUST grab attention. If text hook isn't compelling, no one watches past it.

**Posting Strategy:**

- **Best times to post (UAE timezone, GMT+4):**
  - Morning: 7-9 AM (people commuting, checking phones)
  - Lunch: 12-2 PM (lunch break scroll)
  - Evening: 6-9 PM (after work, peak scroll time)

- **Frequency:** 3-4 Reels/week = Mon/Wed/Fri + optional Sat or Sun

- **Hashtags:** 5 per post. Always lead with #BankingFirstMethod (branded). Add 4 topic-specific tags. Rotate topic tags between posts. Quality over quantity — fewer, more relevant hashtags outperform hashtag stuffing.

- **First comment:** Post CTA link in first comment immediately after publishing (Instagram doesn't allow clickable links in captions, but first comment gets visibility).

- **Engagement:** Respond to ALL comments within first 2 hours (boosts algorithm). Ask a question in caption to encourage comments.

- **Save-bait:** Add "Save this for when you're ready to buy" or "Bookmark this checklist" in caption. Saves = strongest algorithm signal in 2026 (LinkedIn and Instagram both).

**Content Repurposing:**

Each Reel can be repurposed:
1. **Instagram Reel** (primary)
2. **Instagram Story** (repost Reel to Story 24 hours later)
3. **LinkedIn video post** (native upload, not link — higher reach)
4. **YouTube Shorts** (vertical format, same content)
5. **TikTok** (if expanding to TikTok later)

One shoot = 5 distribution channels.
