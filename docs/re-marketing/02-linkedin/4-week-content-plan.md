# WTP Boutique Real Estate — 4-Week LinkedIn Content Plan

> **Campaign Period:** Weeks 1-4 (20 posts)
> **Cadence:** 5 posts/week (Mon-Fri)
> **Objective:** Establish RE positioning, generate partner + investor leads
> **Brand Voice:** Clinical, expert, restrained. Risk/outcome lens. First person.

---

## Algorithm Strategy (360 Brew / 2026)

**3 Core Topics** (80% of content must fall within these):
1. **UAE Banking & Property Integration** — banking-first methodology, SOF, pre-screening
2. **Golden Visa & Tax-Compliant Relocation** — visa mechanics, exit tax, substance
3. **Dubai Investment Intelligence** — market data, area comparisons, ROI analysis

**Format priority** (based on 2026 performance data):
1. Carousels/Infographics — highest follower conversion and saves
2. Document posts — deep content, high save rate
3. Text + story posts — relationship building
4. Video — awareness only

**Branded Frameworks** (WTP intellectual property to repeat):
- **The Banking-First Method™** — pre-screen → structure → buy (not buy → hope for bank)
- **The 3-Question Filter** — Can they bank? What's the exit plan? What's the structure?
- **The Bankable Investor Checklist** — 6-point SOF + eligibility framework

**Hook rule:** First 1-2 sentences get 3-5x algorithm attention. Every hook must be directional (about banking/RE/visa), specific (numbers, scenarios), and pattern-interrupting.

**Save optimization:** Carousels with frameworks, checklists, and comparison data drive saves — the #1 algorithm signal in 2026.

## Content Development Notes

**Voice Consistency:**
- Always first person ("I", "we", "my client")
- Clinical precision: "47% YoY" not "massive growth"
- No hype: "worth considering" not "unmissable opportunity"
- No emojis in body (optional sparingly in CTA line)

**Proof Points to Weave In:**
- WTP corporate track record (banking-first model proven)
- Partner network (already operational)
- Real case numbers (anonymized, percentage-based)
- Regulatory knowledge (Golden Visa updates, compliance)

**What to Avoid:**
- Guaranteed returns
- "Cheapest," "fastest," "easiest"
- Offshore language
- "We'll handle everything" (implies hand-holding, not partnership)
- Stock property photos (if visual needed, use charts/data)
