Succession planning

Who owns the asset today — and who it passes to tomorrow

We are not asking anyone to prepare for death. We are helping them decide, during their lifetime, how everything they have built will carry on.

What can be settled

In advance, and on your terms

  • Beneficiaries and shares — including outside the default distribution
  • Different assets directed to different people
  • An executor to represent the owner's wishes
  • Property, shareholdings, accounts and deposits brought into one logic
Two formats

All property, or specific bequests

  • The whole body of assets — present and future. Suits those who continue to buy and sell
  • Or individual bequests: the apartment to a spouse, the shareholding to a son, the deposit to the children equally
  • Fixed fee: USD 2,725 (AED 10,000)
The limits. We undertake preparation, recording the client's wishes in legal form and handling registration through Dubai Courts. We do not undertake that no succession procedure will arise, or that assets will transfer «in a day». The applicable law is determined by a specialist lawyer.

Liquidation

A company that has stopped trading — not a closed company

An expired licence or the owner leaving the country does not mean the company has gone. Until there is a formal closure and tax deregistration, the obligations remain.

PositionWhat remains in forceSource
Corporate TaxDeregistration within 3 months of cessation. On default: AED 1,000 and monthly thereafter, capped at AED 10,000. Unpaid tax — a penalty equivalent to 14% per annum[1–3]
VATApplication within 20 working days. Closing the licence does not replace VAT deregistration[4]
Licence and registerWhere struck off by the authority, the liability of directors and members continues as though the company had not been dissolved[5]
UBO registerA dormant company must still maintain its record: a warning, then AED 50,000, and AED 100,000 on a repeat breach[6]

As at 28 July 2026. Sources: [1] FTA Decision 6/2023 · [2] Federal Decree-Law 47/2022, Art. 52 · [3] Cabinet Decision 75/2023 · [4] FTA, VAT Deregistration · [5] Federal Decree-Law 32/2021, Art. 309–325 · [6] Cabinet Resolution 132/2023. Consequences depend on the specific case.
