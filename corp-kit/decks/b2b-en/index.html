The economics of a referral

One client handed over is not one transaction

Corporate services in the UAE run in chains. Company formation and the Golden Visa are entry points into a client's lifecycle, not one-off operations.

Entry point

Company formation leads to

  • Establishment card and e-signature
  • Owner and employee visas
  • Personal and corporate bank accounts
  • Licence amendments
  • Annual renewal — every year the company remains alive
Fast mover

The Golden Visa leads to

  • Family visas — spouse and children
  • Personal account and premium banking
  • Wills and powers of attorney
  • A company
  • Mortgage and tax residency certificate, where applicable
Why the Golden Visa suits a partner. A fast, well-defined service with a fixed fee — easy to explain to a client, and the route and quote are known in advance. It also opens the conversation about everything else.

Limits

What we promise — and what we never promise

A partner is putting their reputation on the line. So the list of what we do not say to clients matters more to them than a list of our merits.

What we say

  • The decision on an account is always the bank's, as a matter of law
  • The 0% Corporate Tax rate applies to qualifying income only where the Qualifying Free Zone Person conditions are met; other taxable income may be charged at 9%
  • Relationships with bankers mean understanding requirements and communicating openly — not bypassing KYC
  • Liquidation timelines follow the diagnostic, not the first message
  • The applicable succession law is determined by a specialist lawyer, not by an account manager

What we never say

  • «The account is guaranteed whatever the circumstances»
  • «We have someone at the bank, so there will be no checks»
  • «A free zone means automatic zero tax»
  • «We will close the company in X days» — before the diagnostic
  • We do not conceal previous refusals or assist in circumventing AML/CFT
