How banking risk works

Usually the problem is the picture, not the company

The Central Bank of the UAE (CBUAE) requires banks to understand a company's owners, the nature of its business, the purpose of the account and the source of funds. Where that picture cannot be formed, the bank is entitled not to begin — or to end — the relationship.

Documents and communication

  • The company did not respond to a KYC review, or responded late and incompletely
  • Documents contradict the application form
  • No current licences, contracts or financial statements
  • The bank cannot trace the source of funds or the purpose of transactions

Business profile

  • Operations do not match the licence
  • Actual geography differs from what was declared
  • Turnover or counterparties have changed sharply
  • Complex ownership and an unclear UBO

History and risk markers

  • A previous refusal or account closure
  • Adverse information in open sources
  • Name match against a sanctions or PEP profile
  • High-risk countries, sectors or payment corridors
Worth knowing. One bank's refusal is not an automatic bar everywhere else. But attempting to conceal a refusal, a restriction or the real reason raises the risk of a further refusal — which is why we begin from an honest picture, even an inconvenient one.
Terminology

«Bank blacklist» is a loose term covering three distinct situations

They call for entirely different action, and confusing them is expensive.

01 · Internal risk flag

The bank has previously declined, closed an account or marked the profile as elevated risk. Such a case can sometimes be reworked and directed along a different banking route.

02 · False positive / name match

A name or company has matched another record. This calls for identification, documents and a proper explanation of the differences.

03 · Confirmed sanctions

Where mandatory restrictions genuinely apply, WTP makes no promise of an account and offers no means of circumvention.

Questions

What people ask after being declined

Two banks have already declined me. Is there any point?

Yes — that is precisely where our work begins. One bank's refusal is not an automatic bar everywhere else. What matters is establishing what the bank saw: missing documents, operations not matching the licence, an untraceable source of funds, or a mistaken name match.

What cannot be done is concealing the earlier refusals — that raises the risk of a further one.

What am I paying for?

Not form-filling, and not «an introduction to a banker». A preliminary assessment of viability before payment, selection of a bank suited to the activity and geography, a proper description of the business model, preparation of a document set, work on the history of previous refusals, and coordination with the banker through to opening.

What if the account is not opened after all?

If a case taken on is not carried through to the agreed outcome — provided you have met the agreed conditions and supplied complete and accurate information — WTP's fee is refunded in the manner set out in the engagement agreement.

The provision does not apply where, after the preliminary check, the ownership structure, activity, UBO or transaction profile has changed, or where material facts were not disclosed. All terms are discussed alongside the fee before work begins, not afterwards.

Do you guarantee the account will open?

No. The decision rests with the bank, and that is not a formality: the CBUAE requires it to understand the owners, the nature of the business, the purpose of the account and the source of funds.

We are accountable for the case reaching the bank prepared and defensible, and for your knowing how realistic the route is before you pay rather than after.

Why is the pre-check free?

Because taking on a case with no prospect serves neither side. The preliminary check filters out situations where no lawful and realistic route currently exists, and saves everyone's time.
