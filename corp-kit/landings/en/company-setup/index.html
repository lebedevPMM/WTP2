Jurisdictions

Jurisdiction is the third question, not the first

The same word — «logistics», «IT» or «consultancy» — can map to entirely different activity codes and entirely different requirements. Activity, market, infrastructure, visas and external approvals come first; the zone comes after.

Local market

Mainland Dubai / Abu Dhabi

Trading directly into the local market: shops, restaurants, salons, clinics, construction and field services, local tenders, and a range of regulated professions.

  • Usually requires suitable premises and local approvals
  • Six base licence families: commercial, professional, industrial, tourism, agricultural, crafts
International operations

Free Zone

We work directly in IFZA and RAKEZ; SHAMS, Meydan, DMCC and other zones are assessed and matched to the activity. Suited to international services, IT, consultancy, e-commerce, trading and re-export.

  • We verify the activity, place of performance and invoicing
  • Serving the mainland may require a permit, branch or distributor
  • For trading — importer of record, customs and warehousing
Holding structures

DIFC and ADGM

Financial centres with their own legal regime. Holding companies, SPVs, foundations, family offices and more complex ownership.

  • English common law framework and regulatory recognition
  • Not every such structure can trade or employ staff
  • A passive structure must not be presented as an operating company
Separate track

Regulated activity

Financial services, payments, insurance, securities, virtual assets, legal services, audit, medicine, education and recruitment.

  • A commercial licence alone is not sufficient
  • Regulatory analysis, banking profile and source of funds are addressed before setup
Said plainly

Promises you will not find on this page

Most disappointment in this market originates in wording that sounds attractive at the selling stage and falls apart at the delivery stage.

What we say

  • The 0% Corporate Tax rate applies to qualifying income only where the Qualifying Free Zone Person conditions are met; other taxable income may be charged at 9%
  • The final decision on an account is always the bank's, as a matter of law
  • Relationships with bankers mean understanding requirements and communicating openly — not bypassing KYC
  • General Trading often draws enhanced scrutiny; where the goods are clear, a bank may prefer specific wholesale activities
  • Nationality alone does not determine the outcome — the bank looks at residency, source of funds, business model and documents

What we never say

  • «A free zone means automatic zero tax»
  • «The account is guaranteed whatever the circumstances»
  • «We have someone at the bank, so there will be no checks»
  • «Take General Trading so you can do everything»
  • «Incorporate first, sort the account out later» — where the account is critical to operations

Questions

Questions asked before signing

Why do you not publish a «from» price?

Because for company formation specifically, the quote depends on the activity, the number of visas, premises and approvals, and can differ several times over. An advertised licence price is not the cost of getting a business running.

We give the full figure after ten minutes of questions, and it includes everything mandatory. Where the scope of work is predictable — the banking project, liquidation, visas — we state a starting or fixed fee straight away. If, compared like for like, we turn out to be more expensive than a competitor, we put the calculation forward for review of our professional fee.

Do you guarantee the bank account will open?

No, and nobody can. The decision rests with the bank, which is required by the Central Bank of the UAE (CBUAE) to understand the company's owners, the nature of the business, the purpose of the account and the source of funds.

What we do: before incorporation we put the owner, business cycle, countries and payments to a banker, obtain a view, and build the profile against that bank's requirements. That removes most of the reasons for refusal — it does not replace the bank's decision.

What do your banking relationships actually mean?

Working professional relationships: bankers explain risk appetite and the required documents in advance. No bypassing of KYC and no informal decisions — the bank carries out its full review.

Why is the account billed separately from the company?

Incorporation and bank onboarding are two independent processes with different logic and different timelines. We show them as separate lines so the price is transparent, but we manage them as one project.

I want the cheapest free zone available

We will find an economical option if it permits your activity, the number of visas you need and the banking route. If it does not, today's saving becomes a licence change a few months from now.

And if the bank still declines?

We establish the reason, adjust the file and proceed along the agreed alternative. If the business model has changed or new risks have appeared, the route is reconsidered separately.
