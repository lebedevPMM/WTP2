What you get

Not a document, but clarity and a route already decided

We are not asking anyone to prepare for death. We are helping them decide, during their lifetime, how everything they have built will carry on.

  • Name the beneficiaries yourself and fix that decision in legal form.
  • Set the shares and direct different assets to different people.
  • Appoint an executor to represent the owner's wishes and coordinate the process.
  • Provide for a spouse, children and other family members.
  • Reduce uncertainty for the family — so no one has to reconstruct the owner's wishes from correspondence and conversations.
  • Bring into one logic property, shareholdings, accounts, deposits and other proprietary rights.
Scope

Two formats of coverage

WTP maps the assets and the wishes; a specialist lawyer confirms the correct structure and wording.

Format 1

All present and future property

A will can cover the whole body of assets a person holds now and will acquire later — where those assets fall within the scope of the instrument and the applicable procedure.

  • Suits an investor who continues to buy and sell property
  • Opens accounts and deposits, acquires shareholdings, builds new assets
  • Beneficiaries and shares are settled in advance: for example, children 50/50, a spouse, other family
Format 2

Specific individual bequests

A particular property, a shareholding, a bank deposit or an account can be identified separately, with the person it should pass to named.

  • Suits someone living in one property with no intention of selling
  • Or holding a settled business, or wishing to pass a particular asset to a particular family member
  • For example: the apartment to a spouse, the shareholding to a son, the deposit to the children equally
Circumstances change. Where the family, the assets or the wishes change materially, a will should be revisited. That is a normal part of planning, not a sign that the original document was wrong.

Questions

What people clarify before starting

Who is this product for?

The primary client is an adult non-Muslim holding assets in the UAE. For other situations, the applicable route is determined by a specialist lawyer: we do not draw that conclusion ourselves and do not ask for payment before the review.

Can assets I do not yet hold be covered?

Yes — that is the first of the two formats. A will can cover the whole body of assets held now and acquired later, where those assets fall within the scope of the instrument and the applicable procedure. This suits people who continue to buy and sell property, open accounts and acquire shareholdings.

What happens if the family or the assets change?

Where the family, the asset base or the wishes change materially, the will should be revisited. That is part of ordinary planning.

What is an executor, and must one be appointed?

The executor is the person who represents the owner's wishes and coordinates the process. Appointing one is among the matters that can be settled in advance; the exact arrangement is discussed with the lawyer when the instrument is prepared.

Why is the fee fixed?

Because the scope of work on this product is predictable. We state USD 2,725 (AED 10,000) at the outset and confirm the document set and applicable route before work begins.
