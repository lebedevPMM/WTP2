Risk map

What keeps accruing against an abandoned company

What follows is not scaremongering but a verifiable map with citations to the legislation. The specific consequences depend on the jurisdiction, the corporate form, the tax position, employees, debts and trading history.

Positions that do not close themselves
Position What remains Exposure and penalty Source
Corporate Tax A deregistration application must be filed within 3 months of cessation or liquidation. Before deregistration, all returns including the final one must be filed and any tax and penalties settled. Late deregistration — AED 1,000 on default and monthly thereafter, capped at AED 10,000. Late return — AED 500/month for the first 12 months and AED 1,000/month thereafter. Unpaid tax — a penalty equivalent to 14% per annum, applied monthly. [1–3]
VAT Where an obligation to deregister for VAT arises, the application must be filed within 20 working days. Closing the licence does not replace VAT deregistration. Late deregistration — AED 1,000 on default and monthly thereafter, capped at AED 10,000. Further penalties are possible for unfiled returns, incorrect data and non-payment. [4]
Licence and register An expired licence is not liquidation. The authority may suspend the licence and later strike the company off, but this does not extinguish obligations. Where the company is struck off by the authority, the liability of directors, managers, members and partners continues as though the company had not been dissolved. Liquidation takes effect against third parties only once registered. [5]
UBO register Even a dormant company may remain obliged to maintain and update its beneficial ownership record. For failure to keep the register properly — a warning, then AED 50,000, and AED 100,000 on a repeat breach. [6]
The common misreading. «I am not trading, the account is empty, the licence has expired — so the company must have closed itself.» In fact, until there is a formal closure and tax deregistration, the obligations and the liability remain.

Questions

What owners of dormant companies ask

The licence expired on its own. Has the company not closed?

No. An expired licence is not liquidation. The authority may suspend the licence and later strike the company off the register, but this does not extinguish its obligations: where a company is struck off by the authority, the liability of directors, managers, members and partners continues as though it had not been dissolved.

The company never traded. Do tax obligations still apply?

An absence of operations does not in itself lift registration obligations. If the company was registered for Corporate Tax or VAT, returns and deregistration applications must be filed within the prescribed deadlines — otherwise administrative penalties accrue.

How long will it take?

We do not give a timeline before the diagnostic, and that is a matter of principle. Liquidation involves several authorities and depends on what has accumulated: tax loose ends, open visas, a lease, obligations to creditors. After the diagnostic we give a realistic horizon together with the plan.

Why do so few providers offer this?

Because it is long and labour-intensive, and the client is rarely in a hurry — the company has been abandoned and it appears that nothing is happening. We take it on precisely because the accumulated obligations surface later and cost more.

What do I end up with?

A closure pack: resolutions, certificates, confirmations, final reports and a register of exactly what has been closed. It is a documentary record you can rely on if questions arise years later.
