Four routes

The route follows what you already hold

We start by establishing what is already in place: a property in Dubai, free capital, a planned purchase, or the need for a valid visa and Emirates ID quickly. The route is chosen after that, not before.

10 years

Golden Visa through property

One or several properties in Dubai with a combined confirmed value from approximately USD 545,000 (AED 2,000,000).

  • With a mortgage, we verify the paid portion, the Title Deed and the bank's documents
  • The fee is fixed and does not change if you have held a residence visa or Emirates ID before
  • Property held 50/50 by spouses — Golden Visa for one, sponsored residence visa for the other
10 years

Golden Visa through a deposit

A deposit from approximately USD 545,000 (AED 2,000,000) placed with a Dubai bank for no less than two years.

  • A valid residence visa and Emirates ID are required before the route can start
  • Term, rate, product and release conditions are confirmed by the bank for your case
  • No visa yet — the digital nomad visa first, then the account and the deposit
2 years

Property owner visa

An ordinary residence visa, not a Golden Visa. For an owner whose property does not reach the Golden Visa threshold.

  • Residency in your own right, without setting up a company
  • Issued in roughly 10–14 working days
  • With shared ownership, the applicant's share is guided from approximately USD 109,000 (AED 400,000)
Fast entry

Digital nomad visa

A residency route in its own right — or a quick intermediate step before the deposit route.

  • USD 2,400 (AED 8,800)
  • Provides the valid visa and Emirates ID needed to open an account
  • Eligibility conditions and the document set are checked separately

Questions

What people establish before filing

Why does the two-year visa cost the same as the ten-year one?

Because the fee follows the processing route, not the length of the status. The property route is the same whether it leads to a two-year visa or a ten-year one, which is where the identical USD 4,900 comes from. The deposit route and the digital nomad route are different processes and are priced differently.

What does USD 4,900 cover?

Government fees, the medical examination, medical insurance, the required processing stages, issuance of the Emirates ID and WTP's handling of the case. The figure does not change depending on whether you have held a residence visa or Emirates ID before.

The property is registered to both spouses — what then?

If the combined value is from approximately USD 545,000 (AED 2,000,000) and the shares are equal, the Golden Visa is arranged for one spouse against an attested marriage certificate, and a sponsored residence visa for the other. The route is confirmed once the Title Deed and family documents have been reviewed.

I have no visa or Emirates ID, but I do have capital for a deposit

The deposit route requires a valid visa and Emirates ID before it can start. In that case we arrange the digital nomad visa for USD 2,400 first, then move on to opening the account and the deposit route in Dubai.

What happens to the money after the two years?

The minimum placement period is two years. The specific term, rate, product and release arrangements are obtained from the bank in writing for your case before the route begins. We do not repeat another party's terms as though they were our own commitment.

Do you only work in Dubai?

The fees, timelines and routes on this page apply to Dubai. Property purchases, account opening and comparable matters in other emirates are discussed and quoted separately.
