What we take on

The part that isn't selling

Everything below sits with us, under one contract with you.

  • Developer relationships and contracts. Terms, paperwork and the desk on the developer's side.
  • The deal from booking to settlement. Documents, follow-ups and the chasing that eats your week.
  • Accounting and compliance. Bookkeeping, filings and the KYC/AML side of every transaction.
  • What the client needs after the deal. Company, bank account, Golden Visa, will — handled by our team, and you earn on those too.

What stays with you: the client and the sale. The economics of your own agency, without the overhead of running one.

Questions

Straight questions, straight answers

Is this the same thing as the 90/10 payout?

No, and the names do overlap. This page is the partnership: we carry the operations and you keep 90% of your commission. The payout product is factoring — we advance the commission on a confirmed deal so you are not waiting on the developer. Both can be worded as «90/10», which is exactly why they now live on separate pages.

Do I have to give up my clients?

No. The client relationship is yours and stays yours — we work behind it. That is written into the agreement.

Who am I contracted with?

With WTP directly, under an individual agreement that sets out the split, the scope of what we run and how the deal is reported back to you.

Can I use both the partnership and the factoring?

Yes — they answer different problems and are priced separately. Ask us for both sets of terms and we will show how they combine on your case.
