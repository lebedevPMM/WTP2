## Methodology

Tier-1 advisory commentary (BDO, KPMG, EY, A&M, PwC, Saffery, Eversheds, Hogan Lovells, BSA Law) was used only to corroborate primary sources or interpret transition rules. Internal WTP stats (case counts, decline rates, loss anchors) were not verifiable against any public source; flagged separately. All checks performed 2026-05-12. UK FCA scope was checked against FG24/1 plus expat-advisory commentary. The Council Verdict's three "BLOCKER" judgments (Norway, France, UAE Art. 241) were each retested — Council was correct on Norway and Art. 241, but **Council was wrong on France** (the V2 €800K figure is correct).

## Critical Errors (must-fix before any publish)

### #5 Tax Residency — Norway threshold

**Current copy (V2):** *"Norway taxes unrealised gains over five hundred thousand kroner."*
**Reality:** The NOK 500,000 figure was a proposal of 20 March 2024 that was withdrawn. The 2025 National Budget (effective post-7 Oct 2024) replaced it with a **NOK 3 million** basic allowance.
**Replacement copy:** *"Norway taxes unrealised gains on shareholdings above three million kroner."*

### #5 Tax Residency — France threshold (Council Verdict was WRONG here)

**Council Verdict said:** France EUR 800K is misleading; correct is EUR 2.57M.
**Reality:** **The €800,000 figure is correct** as the trigger for Article 167 bis CGI exit tax. €2.57M is a separate threshold that determines whether the income-tax portion of the deferred exit tax can be relieved after 2 years (<€2.57M) or 5 years (>€2.57M). Either threshold supports the V2 line.
**Action:** Keep V2 wording as-is. **Override the Council Verdict blocker on this point.**

### #8 Last Will — Article 241 / Waqf citation

**Current copy (V2):** *"Article 241 of the Personal Status Law, in force January 2026, sets the default. For non-Muslim expats without a registered will, the estate moves under Waqf rules."*
**Reality:** Federal Decree-Law 41/2022 has 18 articles (not 241). The operative provision for intestate succession of non-Muslims is **Article 11** of Law 41/2022. The 2024 PSL (Decree-Law 41/2024 effective 15 April 2025) applies to Muslims and does not change the non-Muslim regime. "Waqf rules" is wrong terminology — Waqf is the Islamic charitable-endowment regime; the default for non-Muslims absent an election is the Civil Personal Status framework (Art. 11), and Sharia rules may still apply depending on forum.
**Replacement copy:** *"Without a registered will in the UAE, a non-Muslim expat's estate may default to home-country law or Sharia principles depending on the forum and the asset. A DIFC or Dubai Courts will fixes the forum and the distribution in advance."*

### #11 Golden Visa — AED 17,000 "government fee"

**Current copy (V2):** *"Government fee is AED seventeen thousand."*
**Reality:** Pure government fees for a 10-year Golden Visa run AED ~2,800–4,500 (visa) + AED 1,150 (Emirates ID) + medical/admin. All-in govt-side cost lands AED 9,000–15,000 depending on emirate and category. AED 17,000 is consistent with a bundled agency retainer.
**Replacement copy:** *"All-in cost typically AED 9,000-15,000 — government, medical, Emirates ID. Our retainer sits on top of that."*

### #6 Accounting & Compliance — ESR reference

**Current copy (V2):** *"ESR notification six months after year-end, report six months after that."*
**Reality:** ESR has been repealed for FYs from 1 Jan 2023 by Cabinet Resolution 98/2024 (published Sept 2024). ESR filings are required only for historical 2019-2022 periods. Substance is now embedded inside Corporate Tax obligations for Qualifying Free Zone Persons. The "6+6 month notification/report cycle" is now legacy.
**Replacement copy:** *"Substance — folded into Corporate Tax filings since 2023 for Free Zone entities seeking the 0% qualifying rate."*

### #19 Dutch Box 3 — 2026 reset claim

**Current copy (V2):** *"Parameter changes are scheduled for 2026."*
**Reality:** 2026 carries the rebuttal regime ("tegenbewijsregeling box 3") — taxpayers can report actual return if lower than notional. The full reform ("Wet werkelijk rendement box 3") targets 1 January **2028**, was passed by the House in Nov 2025, and is now pending Senate.
**Replacement copy:** *"The rebuttal regime applies through 2027. A full real-return reform is set for 2028, currently pending Senate approval."*

## FCA Specific Note (Reel #17)

**FG24/1 prominence requirements:**

The FCA's [Finalised Guidance FG24/1 on financial promotions on social media](https://www.fca.org.uk/publications/finalised-guidance/fg24-1-finalised-guidance-financial-promotions-social-media) (issued March 2024) is media-neutral and applies to any communication that could constitute a financial promotion targeted at UK consumers. Three points are load-bearing for Reel #17:

1. **Standalone compliance.** Every individual post must comply when viewed alone. Risk warnings cannot sit behind a "see more" or click-through. A 2-second disclaimer at sec 48-50 on a 50-sec reel does not meet this standard for prominence — the FCA explicitly criticises "obscuring or partly truncating key information."
2. **Prominence and timing.** FG24/1 cites behavioural research that risk warnings are more effective at or just before the relevant content. A disclaimer surfacing only in the final 2 seconds, when most viewers have already swiped, is structurally weak.
3. **Scope question — is Reel #17 actually a financial promotion?** This is the crucial gating question. **Pure tax advisory is outside the FCA's perimeter** — the FCA has explicitly stated it does not regulate accountants or tax advisors. However, the reel discusses "trusts, accounts, and holding companies" being re-examined; if the underlying service WTP markets to UK residents involves arranging or advising on regulated investments (e.g., structured lending, securities-based Lombard lines, fund placement via UAE entities), then yes — Section 21 FSMA bites and FG24/1 applies. If WTP markets only tax-residency mechanics + DIFC wills + foundation registration, the FCA perimeter likely does not apply, but advertising standards (ASA / BCAP) and UK consumer-protection law still do.

**Practical recommendation for #17:**
- (a) Move the disclaimer to a **persistent 50%-bottom overlay for the full 50 seconds**, ≥24pt-equivalent. Not a flash at the end.
- (b) Add the standard FCA-style risk wording where applicable: *"This is for general information and is not financial, tax, or legal advice. Capital is at risk."* — even if the FCA may not technically apply, this is defensible content.
- (c) **Commission a UK partner counsel call** before posting. Two open questions only UK counsel can answer: (i) does WTP's full service offering bring it inside Section 21 FSMA for UK-targeted IG content? (ii) if yes, does WTP need an authorised approver under the Financial Promotions Approval Regime?
- (d) Fallback: **geo-exclude the UK** from the audience targeting for this specific reel. Removes FCA exposure without killing the asset. If this is the chosen route, ensure Meta audience settings actually exclude UK — and that organic shares to UK users are flagged.
