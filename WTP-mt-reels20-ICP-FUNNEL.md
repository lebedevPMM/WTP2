## Inbound Routing Concerns

**Keyword integrity (per V2 CLEAN line 16):** all 20 keywords are UPPERCASE single-word and unique except the intentional PRESCREEN double-use on #1 + #20.

**Cleanly automatable (single-word, no collision with English noise):**
PRESCREEN, BANKING, PREMIUM, COMPANY, ACCOUNTING, REJECT, WILLS, FOUNDATION, WEALTH, GOLDEN, RESIDENCY, ESCROW, XRAY, FACTORING, GERMANY, NETHERLANDS.

**Risk keywords (high collision with normal DM language):**
- **TAX** (#5) — extremely common word, will pick up off-topic DMs. Recommend `TAXRES` or `TWO-COUNTRIES`.
- **RE** (#16) — two-letter token, will be matched by "RE: subject line" replies and auto-quotes. **High-priority fix.** Recommend `REALESTATE` or `RESEQ`.
- **UK** (#17) — common geo-code in IG DMs. Acceptable, but ManyChat rule needs to require exact-match standalone token.
- **COMPANY** (#4) — common word. Acceptable but flag.

**PRESCREEN dual-use (#1 + #20):** Council and V2 constitution both confirm this is by design — same funnel destination. **Confirmed: this serves the funnel.** Both reels route to the same intake form, so duplicate-keyword routing is not a problem (it is the desired outcome). The 13-position posting gap (Mon-W1 → Fri-W3) protects against feed fatigue. Recommend ManyChat send a single intake response regardless of which reel triggered it, but include a "Saw it twice?" line in the auto-reply for the small overlap audience — turns the repetition into selectivity signal rather than mistake.

**Lead-magnet completeness:** Of 20 reels, 18 promise a specific document or intake form. Two are softer ("we send the onboarding map this week" #3; "for terms" #15). For #3 PREMIUM specifically — recommend tightening to a same-day deliverable to match the cadence the other reels set ("we send the bank-match shortlist today").

**Conversion-path dead-end check:** Every reel routes DM → intake/form → microlanding → discovery call. No dead ends. The three jurisdiction-specific spokes (uk-non-dom, german-exit-tax, dutch-box3) already exist as routes per `LanguageContext.tsx` — good. Verify each spoke has a working consultation booking before posting #17/#18/#19.

## Persona-Audience Concerns

**Oleg (Cluster A, 7 reels — Banking & Setup).** Operational banking-expert voice matches HNWI expectations for banking-first content. Persona-fit is the strongest in the batch. No reassignments needed.

**Olga (Cluster B, 6 reels — Legal / Estate / Visa).** Legal-precision voice is appropriate for wills, foundations, escrow, residency. Persona-fit clean. No reassignments needed.

**AI avatar (Clusters C+D, 7 reels — Capital, RE, Market Spokes, Concept).** This is the **structural concern of the batch.**

- For #14 X-Ray ($100M+ / 15 families claim) and #17/18/19 (UK-ND, DE-XT, NL-B3 — the three highest-ticket HNWI segments in the deck), HNWI viewers making £5–50M decisions are statistically the most credibility-sensitive cohort. An AI-rendered face delivering "leaving the UK does not leave the UK" or "Wegzugsteuer §6 AStG, 7-year installment" risks reading as "AI-generated content marketing" rather than "this firm has a partner who specializes in this." On YouTube ad/IG-feed contexts, the recognition cost compounds: AI avatars are now identifiable within 2–3 seconds by sophisticated viewers, and the very audience WTP wants is the most trained.
- For #16 Real Estate Strategic Tool and #20 Pre-Screen concept, the avatar risk is lower because the message is product-mechanic (escrow, sequence, decline rate) rather than jurisdictional-legal advice.

**Specific recommendation: switch #17, #18, #19 to a human specialist** — ideally Oleg for #18 DE (operational banking-input frame), and a yet-to-cast UK partner counsel and NL partner counsel for #17 and #19 respectively. The council already flagged a UK-counsel sign-off is mandatory for #17 due to FCA FG24/1 — that legal call is the natural moment to ask whether the UK partner will also appear on camera. For #19 NL, V2 copy explicitly names "NL partner firm on the Dutch side" — putting that partner on screen would simultaneously solve credibility and the substance-of-claim issue.

**#14 X-Ray:** keep AI avatar but downscale the $100M anchor to a more defensible "X capital reviewed across our diagnostic book" framing — council already flagged this needs verification anyway.
